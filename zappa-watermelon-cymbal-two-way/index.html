Watermelon In Easter Hay: the cymbal notation does not track the cymbal stem

A two-direction audio check of tab s35881, run against a circular-shift null, three positive controls and a five-stem negative control.

Built 2026-09-07 15:00 CDT · anchor clock: 97 snare anchors, piecewise linear, span 59.3s to 506.1s · tolerance 45 ms

The finding

  • 1,778 notated cymbal events sit inside the scored span, and they match the cymbal stem at 1.14x chance forward and 1.27x chance backward. Both sit under the 1.5x floor this project uses for a real effect.
  • The same method scores snare notation against the snare stem at 5.74x and 5.28x. Kick reaches 3.00x, toms 3.67x. The method finds correspondence where correspondence exists.
  • The notated cymbal lane matches the SNARE stem better than its own. 2.14x against snare, 1.14x against cymbals. That is the shape of events written onto the backbeat grid rather than transcribed from audible cymbal strokes.
  • This is not a ceiling artifact. The dense notated set lifts the null, and there is still headroom to 6.0x forward and 3.1x backward. The lane reached 1.14x and 1.27x.

What it does NOT license

  • It does not justify leaving the ghost marks stripped. The 1,317 anti-accent flags are the original author's own notation. Stripping another contributor's marks was wrong on provenance grounds, and that stays true whether or not the lane tracks the audio.
  • It is a fact about the tab's transcription quality, not a licence to edit. No cymbal event was added, removed or moved on the strength of this measurement.
  • The upload still repairs real damage. The live tab currently shows all 9 track names as empty strings, and the verified file carries all 137 CDATA blocks.

The chord question, answered

The chord did not vanish through a new transcription decision. The saved original carries 8 chord elements and the live tab carries 6, so two were lost on the tab. TEXTSAFE-v2 already restores both, and its chord-definition ids and beat-level chord references show an empty symmetric difference against the saved original. The separate chord patch is redundant and poisoned: 7 chords rather than 8, and zero CDATA.

What this did not test

  • Whether Demucs separation leaks snare energy into the cymbal stem strongly enough to explain the 2.14x snare result. The bleed direction was checked and snare-vs-cymbals scores 2.32x, so bleed is present and real.
  • Individual cymbal events. Every figure here is a rate over the whole span, and no single stroke was adjudicated.
  • Hi-hat lanes, held out of the cymbal class deliberately.

Recommended is used nowhere on this page. Every number above is measured, and the scripts and JSON that produced them are named in the method block.
