The defect nobody had finished counting

A snare cannot be struck loudly and quietly at one instant. A kick cannot be struck twice at one instant either. The live staff carried sixty-three positions that asked for exactly that, each one a voice-1 note landing on the identical bar, position and drum as a voice-0 note.

The earlier pass found forty-nine of them and stopped at the snare. Sweeping every drum instead of one found twelve more on the kick and two on the high mid tom.

The provenance test

The question is whether the doubled note belongs to the original transcriber or arrived in our own later editing. Revision r7715683 answers it directly. That file holds 2,091 drum notes, every one in voice 0, across 193 distinct snare positions with zero stacked. At all sixty-three of the disputed positions it writes one plain stroke and nothing else.

Unanimous, 63 of 63. No position had a ghost there. No position had two notes there. The duplicate is ours in every single case, so removing it restores the author's staff rather than editing it.

Why the coda stays empty

The CDATA gate earned its place

The first build went through ElementTree and came out with zero CDATA wrappers. That is the exact state measured to blank every track name on import: RESTORED-s35881.gp carried 0 CDATA and lost 9 of 9 names on the original tab. The build was redone as text surgery on the raw GPIF so all 137 wrappers survive untouched.
