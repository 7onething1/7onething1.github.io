Main inventory, one row per song

Three albums, 37 tracks, taken in album order from the MusicBrainz release for each record. Both yes-or-no columns are scoped to the album take: a live performance never counts as coverage of the studio recording. The AI transcription is linked whenever one exists of the album take and carries a drum part. A human-made tab is linked only when no AI album-take transcription exists. Live tabs get their own column, and every human tab stays available in the reference table below.

How a "no" is earned here. A drum transcription counts only when a drum staff holds real notes, rests excluded. A tab whose drum staff exists and holds nothing but rests is recorded as an empty staff, never as a drum transcription. A tab that could not be read is recorded as unavailable in search gaps, never as a "no".

Correction, 5 September. A first version of this page counted a tab as AI when aiGenerated OR createdVia said so. Across 49 tabs that union changed exactly one row, and it changed it on the weaker signal. s412177 Cosmik Debris reads aiGenerated=false with createdVia=AI, and it carries prevRevisionId 2010328 and 9,376 views, so it is a long-standing tab whose latest revision was submitted through the AI route. The two fields answer different questions: whether the tab originated as AI, and how its current revision was produced. aiGenerated is now the authority for this column, and the revision route is carried beside it. The effect was that a 1,581-note album-take drum tab was scored as AI and never reached this reference table.
Why the version column matters. A human tab of a live take is not a check on an AI tab of the studio take. Where the tab titles itself as a live or excerpt upload, that is recorded. Where nothing in the tab identifies the take, the column says so rather than assuming the album version.

Method

Tab data comes from Songsterr's own JSON with no browser and no login: the meta API for provenance and revision, and the CloudFront notation path for the notes themselves. Note counts exclude rests, since Songsterr emits a {"rest": true} object inside each beat's note list. A song counts as having a drum transcription only when a drum staff holds real notes.

Album track order, titles and lengths come from the MusicBrainz release for each record. Per-track drum credits come from MusicBrainz recording-level artist relationships, which is why the evidence links point at a recording rather than at an album page.

The duration arithmetic behind the version column was validated against two published tabs and reproduced their figures to the hundredth of a second. Details are on the per-album comparison pages.
