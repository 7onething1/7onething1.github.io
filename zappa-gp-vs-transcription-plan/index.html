The blocker on this plan is source coverage, not tab quality.

Twenty-four of twenty-six songs cannot be checked today, because no professional drum chart for them exists in the archive. Any row claiming a mismatch on those songs would be invented, so every one of them reads not yet measured.

Zappa Analysis cannot vote on drums, and this corrects an assumption in the archive's own coverage file.

_raw/coverage.json credits 18 of these songs with 2 to 11 notation images from Kasper Sloots' Zappa Analysis. I opened 05-zappa-analysis/zomby-woof2.jpg and it is a pitched score: treble and bass clefs, vocal line with lyrics, two keyboard staves, bass in 8vb. There is no drum staff on the page. Those pages settle meter and barring, and nothing about a drum lane.

How each row was decided

Songsterr ids come from the live search API, filtered to Frank Zappa, with the drum track counted by instrumentId 1024. Transcription coverage comes from a directory listing of the archive, not from its index file, because those files are iCloud dataless placeholders that find and mdfind under-report.

One professional transcription is enough to correct a tab. The Songsterr side is a machine guess with a documented failure mode, so a printed chart is not held to a two-of-three vote against it. Two charts are needed only when they disagree with each other.

3. Roxy & Elsewhere (1974)

Searched 2026-09-05, and the answer is worse than a missing download. No published drum transcription of any Roxy track appears to exist.

  • zappa-analysis.com's own catalogue of published Zappa scores names no drum book at all. Its list runs to Barfko Swill full scores, Kai Schnell, Guitar Player Magazine solos, Wolfgang Ludwig's study, Paul Pappas, and roughly 300 pages of Steve Vai guitar transcriptions. Nothing for drums.
  • Cruise Ship Drummer's Chester Thompson page carries one transcription, Gibraltar, from Weather Report's Black Market. Thompson is the Roxy drummer and Todd Bishop has not transcribed him on this album.
  • DRUM! Magazine's Groove Analysis series covers Who Are The Brain Police, The Grand Wazoo and Zomby Woof, plus the seven pages already on disk. No Roxy title surfaced.
  • zappa-analysis.com does hold transcriptions of Pygmy Twylyte, by Wolfgang Ludwig from 1992 with 1974 guitar bars added in 2007, and of Echidna's Arf. Both are pitched score, which cannot vote on a drum lane.

So the closed layer is publication, not access.

The route for Roxy is not another download. It is transcribing the drums from the record, which makes every Roxy row a transcription job rather than a comparison job, and a different kind of work from every other song in this plan.

Method each execution chat follows

  1. Identify the passage on three measures before comparing anything: meter, tempo and event count. Tempo can be measured off the record with librosa.beat.beat_track even where the record cannot settle a single note.
  2. Measure lanes, do not read them. Counting the bottom staff line as lane 0, kick is lane 0.5 and snare lane 2.5. tools/chart_heads.py isolates heads by erosion.
  3. Build the subdivision grid from the hi-hat positions, never by dividing the bar width, which misassigns notes at the ends.
  4. Compare slot by slot. A high agreement rate validates the alignment. A low one means the passages do not correspond and the comparison is void.
  5. Verify against the author's own pre-damage revision as a set difference on (bar, slot, drum, flag), not as a count. A count going up proves nothing.
