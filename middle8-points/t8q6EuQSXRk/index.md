1. **[00:00:24](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8q6EuQSXRk&t=24s)** The Dare, Harrison Patrick Smith, draws LCD Soundsystem comparisons on four specific counts: squelchy synths, claps on the backbeat, a half-drunk James Murphy vocal impression, and album art that echoes LCD's.
2. **[00:01:05](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8q6EuQSXRk&t=65s)** The borrowing is wider than one band. Electroclash, new rave, blog house and dance punk are named as the tangled web he pulls from, each one a collision of punk energy, electronic gear and no interest in being respectable.
3. **[00:02:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8q6EuQSXRk&t=162s)** Before this he made anxious indie rock in Portland under the name Turtleneck. Girls was written in under an hour during lockdown as a reaction against music he thought had become too smooth and too serious.
4. **[00:03:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t8q6EuQSXRk&t=183s)** He uploaded it in August 2022 and TikTok did the rest, while he was still a substitute teacher by day and DJing Lower East Side parties by night.
