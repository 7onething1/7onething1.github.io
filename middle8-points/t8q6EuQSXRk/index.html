  1. The Dare, Harrison Patrick Smith, draws LCD Soundsystem comparisons on four specific counts: squelchy synths, claps on the backbeat, a half-drunk James Murphy vocal impression, and album art that echoes LCD's.

  2. The borrowing is wider than one band. Electroclash, new rave, blog house and dance punk are named as the tangled web he pulls from, each one a collision of punk energy, electronic gear and no interest in being respectable.

  3. Before this he made anxious indie rock in Portland under the name Turtleneck. Girls was written in under an hour during lockdown as a reaction against music he thought had become too smooth and too serious.
