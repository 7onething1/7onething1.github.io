# Every time the Arctic Monkeys changed their sound

Middle 8 &middot; 14:37 &middot; 2,298 words of transcript

Video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXzaUFYgfVc

1. **[00:00:29](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXzaUFYgfVc&t=29s)** Three albums are named as the actual hinges across seven records: Humbug, AM, and Tranquility Base Hotel & Casino. Everything else is adjustment between them.
2. **[00:01:17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXzaUFYgfVc&t=77s)** The sequencing on the first two records is already deliberate. Riot Van sits at the centre of the debut as a breather, and Favourite Worst Nightmare eases its tempo track by track from Brianstorm down to Only Ones Who Know.
3. **[00:02:03](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXzaUFYgfVc&t=123s)** 505 carries Turner's first use of a keyboard, which the video treats as the small opening that the whole later career walks through.
4. **[00:02:25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXzaUFYgfVc&t=145s)** The Last Shadow Puppets, with Miles Kane, James Ford and Owen Pallett, put Turner in front of the 22-piece London Metropolitan Orchestra a decade before Tranquility Base.
5. **[00:03:10](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXzaUFYgfVc&t=190s)** Humbug happened because Josh Homme invited them to his desert studio after they met on tour, at a point where Turner says they had tunes but needed someone to help them crack the code.
6. **[00:03:28](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXzaUFYgfVc&t=208s)** The listening behind it was Nick Cave, Black Sabbath riffs and Creedence Clearwater Revival, and they worked at recreating a feel from Susie Q without copying it.
7. **[00:04:27](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXzaUFYgfVc&t=267s)** The Jeweller's Hands is the first thing Turner wrote at a piano, and he describes it as deliberately placed last, pointing at where the band would go next.
8. **[00:07:09](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXzaUFYgfVc&t=429s)** AM's brief was explicit: apply a hip hop or R&B producer's compositional thinking to a four-piece rock band and make a record that sounds good in a car, which is why bass and drums were laid down first.
9. **[00:08:14](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXzaUFYgfVc&t=494s)** Michael Chapman's 1970 album Fully Qualified Survivor set the mood Turner wanted on AM, and emulating him is likely why his Yorkshire accent is least audible on that record.
10. **[00:10:22](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sXzaUFYgfVc&t=622s)** Tranquility Base exists because of a birthday present. Turner had no ideas until his manager gave him a Steinway Vertegrand for his 30th, and the record was built on it, with Fellini's 8 1/2 and Jean-Pierre Melville's jazz clubs shaping the writing.
