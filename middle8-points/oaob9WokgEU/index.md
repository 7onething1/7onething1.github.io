2. **[00:01:07](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oaob9WokgEU&t=67s)** The record was built during an 18-month fight with liver cancer, and producer Tony Visconti's summary is the line the whole video rests on: his death was no different from his life, a work of art.

7. **[00:08:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oaob9WokgEU&t=522s)** The sessions were run for improvisation and the improvisation survived. The players say everything they did is audible on the finished record, which is why it reads as a rock album transformed by jazz rather than a jazz album.
