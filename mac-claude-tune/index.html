1. The disk is the emergency

10 / 10

932 GB volume with 7.7 GB left

3. The skill-description tax

9 / 10

20,507 tokens of skill frontmatter, every turn, forever

There are 167 skill folders, 160 with a SKILL.md, and 140 carrying frontmatter. Only the body of a skill is free. The frontmatter description is injected into every turn whether the skill is used or not.

SKILLS=160 FRONTMATTER_BYTES=82031 APPROX_TOKENS=20507 description bytes: total=75692 median=495 max=1422 descriptions over 300 bytes: 115 (~17,449 tokens) trimming those 115 to 200 bytes saves ~11,699 tokens EVERY turn
source: python scan of ~/.claude/skills/*/SKILL.md

The ten heaviest descriptions run 900 to 1,461 bytes each: rock-analysis-canon, chavez-radiohead-failure-craft, jazz-harmony-craft, chiron-intuition, engineering-the-guitar, mixing-engineering-craft, rock-form-canon, counterpoint-craft, orchestration-craft, mtg-price-verify.

A description only has to do one job: help pick the right skill. Two sentences and the trigger words do that. Everything else belongs in the body, where it costs nothing until the skill is invoked.

7. What is already healthy

pass

Three areas that need no work

RAM is fine. 74% free with 131 MB of swap used out of 1 GB. There is no memory pressure problem here, so a RAM upgrade would buy nothing.

Shell startup is fast. 0.01 s for an interactive zsh, so your profile adds nothing measurable to Bash tool calls.

Spotlight is enabled and idle, Time Machine is not running, and the Trash is empty. None of the usual background-indexing suspects are the cause.
