# Printed charts we own and never applied

Twelve charts on disk. Three used. Nine idle. 2026-09-06.

**Every applied chart is on one song.** Ryan Brown, Daniel Bedard and Drumnet were all used
on Zomby Woof, cited by name in revisions r8905411 and r8905491. Across every other song in
the collection, a professional transcription has sat on disk and no revision has ever cited
it. This is the worklist, ordered by how far the tab sits from the chart.

## Method

The chart inventory comes from the local archive, counted after deduplicating the
byte-identical filenames. A chart counts as applied when a Songsterr revision on the
matching tab names its source in the description, checked by pulling
`/api/meta/<songId>/revisions` for every affected tab and searching the full description
text for Ryan Brown, DRUM Magazine, Groove Analysis, Bedard, Drumnet, Sweeney or Bishop.
Tab lane counts come from the CloudFront notation JSON, read off the `fret` field against
the General MIDI drum map.
