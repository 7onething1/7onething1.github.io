Method

The chart inventory comes from the local archive at ~/Projects/_outputs/zappa-drum-sources/, counted after deduplicating the byte-identical filenames. A chart counts as applied when a Songsterr revision on the matching tab names its source in the description, checked by pulling /api/meta/<songId>/revisions for every affected tab and searching the full description text for Ryan Brown, DRUM Magazine, Groove Analysis, Bedard, Drumnet, Sweeney or Bishop. Tab lane counts come from the CloudFront notation JSON, read off the fret field against the General MIDI drum map.
