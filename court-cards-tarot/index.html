What are court cards?

Tarot has 16 special "people" cards. There are four kinds of person, the Page (a kid learning), the Knight (a teen on the go), the Queen (a caring grown-up), and the King (a leader). Each one comes in four flavors, Wands (energy), Cups (feelings), Swords (thinking), and Pentacles (money and stuff). Open any card to read what Kate says, and look for the Kid version note for the quick idea.
