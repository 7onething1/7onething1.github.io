02The five corrections that matter most

Fourteen corrections are recorded in the workbook. These five changed either what the project is, whether a funding source applies, or how much can be asked for.

01

The greenhouse was missing from the whole package

WasThe August 6 workbook described an outdoor bed garden. The word greenhouse did not appear on any sheet, even though the earlier proposal was built entirely around upgrading an existing greenhouse.
NowThe greenhouse is named across the budget, the narrative, the metrics, and the procurement specification, and it has its own irrigation zone on the controller.

The July 22 session recorded that the greenhouse already stands and needs exactly these upgrades. An enclosed structure dries faster than open beds, so leaving it off the irrigation plan left out the growing space with the highest watering demand.

02

The site address was contradicted between the two drafts

WasThe July 22 proposal placed KVC Niles in Raytown, in Eastern Jackson County. The August 6 workbook used 1911 E. 23rd St., Kansas City.
NowKansas City, Missouri 64127, confirmed against the KVC Missouri location page and the federal SAM entity record for The Niles Home for Children.

This matters twice. It puts the site inside Kansas City city limits, which the PLANT program requires for eligibility. It also puts the site outside the service area of the grant the earlier proposal was built around.

03

The $30,000 anchor grant does not apply to this site

WasThe Eastland Giving Circle was carried as a $30,000 upfront anchor that could fund nearly the entire greenhouse project on its own.
NowRecorded as not eligible on geography, kept on the Funding Roadmap with the reason attached rather than deleted.

Eastland covers Eastern Jackson County east of I-435, suburban Jackson County outside Kansas City, and Cass County. The site sits inside Kansas City and west of I-435. It was carried only because of the Raytown error. It stays on the sheet as a closed question so nobody rebuilds the plan around it in six months.

04

A required assessment was treated as an optional phone call

WasKansas City Community Gardens appeared as a funding partner to call about grant programs.
NowRaised to a leadership decision due August 11, and flagged as the longest lead time in the package.

The City requires that sites working with KCCG complete a KCCG site assessment before applying and attach it to the application. With 46 days to the deadline, that decision cannot sit in a queue.

05

The award-history question is answered, and the answer is $10,000

WasConfirming whether this site had a prior PLANT award was the first action item, owned by finance, due August 10, with the entire budget contingent on the answer.
NowSettled by evidence. No prior award exists, so the $10,000 request stands and the contingency is closed.

The City publishes its 2025 recipients, all 26 of them, and the launch release confirms 2025 was the first cycle, which makes that list complete rather than partial. KVC appears nowhere on it. The list also carries The Ozanam Therapeutic Teaching Farm, a therapeutic farm at a Kansas City youth residential treatment campus, which is the closest possible precedent for this application.

Also corrected

A cited source URL that returns 404 was marked dead and replaced. Four City requirements that were missing were added to the timeline: the August 25 applicant webinar, a second required grower-support workshop during the grant period, three progress check-ins, and an in-person site visit at the halfway point. The budget was rebuilt from eight round numbers into a component specification. The full list of twelve sits on the What Changed sheet.

04The cash gate, and where it binds

KVC cannot pay project costs and wait to be repaid. PLANT pays half on award, so purchasing runs in two batches and no batch is ordered before its cash arrives.

StageAmountCoversGate
First deposit$5,000 50 percent, paid on award after the agreement is executed Confirmed rule
Batch 1, planned$6,300 Water source connection, greenhouse zone, outdoor zones, controller and filtration Exceeds deposit by $1,300
Second payment$5,000 Released per the grant agreement, terms still to be obtained in writing Terms needed
Batch 2$3,700 Installation and testing, repairs, soil and season extension, tools and harvest supplies Follows payment two
The $1,300 gap is real, and it is shown rather than hidden

The irrigation backbone genuinely costs more than half the grant. Three ways close it, none of them KVC cash: move the greenhouse zone into Batch 2, have KCCG cover the water connection through the KC Grow program, or have a donor pay a vendor directly for one named item. The earlier draft assigned Batch 1 exactly $5,000 with no check that the items matched the deposit, which is how a project stalls in month two.
