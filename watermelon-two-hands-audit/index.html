What the diff screenshot actually was

The preview page was Brandon's own editor change sitting on top of my upload r8968036. It published one minute later as r8968524, described "Fixed tom fills and rolls", at 17:43:08Z on 2026-09-08.

Diffing the two drum payloads measure by measure, exactly 2 measures differ: index 24 and 26. Songsterr's diff view labels systems by measure INDEX, so those print as 24 and 26 in the screenshot and display as bars 25 and 27 in the tab. The measure between them is byte-identical, matching the un-highlighted middle system.

What Brandon changed by hand

  • Index 24, drum voice. A 32nd-note flurry with High Tom and Hi-Mid Tom stacked on one instant became plain sixteenths walking down Hi-Mid, Low-Mid, Low Floor.
  • Index 24, cymbal voice. Twenty unbroken ride sixteenths became an eighth, a sixteenth rest, and a Low-Mid Tom written into that voice.
  • Index 26, cymbal voice. Three snare strokes added at sixteenths 14, 15 and 16, two of them ghosts. The drum voice was left alone.

Why my upload had it wrong

1. Tom positions came from stem onsets and were never snapped to the chart's sixteenth

The printed Drumnet chart engraves at a sixteenth. My plan carried raw onset positions. In index 24 the ten planned onsets sat at 11.23, 11.55, 11.94, 12.85, 13.78, 14.74, 15.73, 16.01, 16.45 and 18.63 sixteenths. Two pairs are closer than a third of a sixteenth, so the notation writer subdivided to 32nds and stacked two toms on one instant.

Across the 40 tom-carrying measures, 30 hold a placement more than 0.20 of a sixteenth off grid and 14 hold two toms closer than three quarters of a sixteenth.

2. The cymbal voice was never re-marked against the fill

The chart pass wrote into the drum voice only, so the cymbal voice kept running. 21 measures of the live head have a cymbal voice with zero rests while three or more toms play underneath. The worst is index 56 with 11 toms and 11 cymbal strokes landing on the same sixteenths.

3. The missing snares are the chart reader's recorded blind spot

That read found 154 snare heads against the tab's 193, and the limit is already written into reference_watermelon_drumnet_chart_read.md. Busy measures lose heads.

4. Low-Mid Tom and Low Floor Tom were never available to the pipeline

The chart carries three tom lanes, mapping to High Tom, Hi-Mid Tom and High Floor Tom. A fill that walks down four drums cannot come out of that map.
