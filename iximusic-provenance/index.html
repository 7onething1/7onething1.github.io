Each sentence on the 17 deep sheets was checked against the transcript of the ixi video it cites. Two questions get two columns. Origin asks who said it first: ixi, Claude, or an ambiguous caption that Claude made specific. Status asks whether anything independent confirms it. How the inventions happen

Thirteen mechanisms account for every Claude-originated or distorted sentence. Counts are sentences tagged; one sentence can carry two mechanisms.

Who wrote it, and where the idea came from

These are two different questions. Claude wrote every score and every deep-sheet sentence, so Claude is the author of every artifact. The idea inside a sentence can still come from ixi. Let Down shows the difference: Claude's score labels the A as an escape tone, the term is ixi's, and the Songsterr capture plus the vocal stem both give G#3, A3, F#3, which is the escape-tone shape. An earlier report from the other audit chat said all 23 sheet errors were Claude's; that line counted authorship and skipped origin. This page keeps the two apart.
