What was actually wrong

Twenty Appleseed Cast .gp5 files were built from the DrumSep 5-piece splits and they are good drum transcriptions. Every one of them contains exactly one track, Drums. Read straight out of the files with PyGuitarPro:

Uploading a one-track file as a revision to an existing Songsterr tab is what removed the guitars and bass from the page. The four saved part files under _outputs/songsterr-lost-tracks/ are those displaced parts: Lead Guitar, Rhythm Guitar, Bass, Drums.

The tempo that did not survive checking

The drum build and the human tab disagreed on tempo, so the audio settled it. The drum stem 04 Mare Mortis-drums.wav is 208.6 seconds long.

SourceTempoBarsImplied lengthError vs 208.6 s
Songsterr human tab125111213.1 s+2.2%
Our stem build136114201.2 s−3.5%

The build's own confidence record says the same thing. 04-Mare-Mortis.tempo.json reports subsets_agreeing: 0 out of 2, phase_error: 0.237 and backbeat: 0.229 against a random_baseline of 0.25. The backbeat score sat below chance, so 136 BPM was never a confident reading.

The drums were re-detected from the same DrumSep 5-piece split on the 125 BPM grid, which puts both halves of the file on one clock.

The drum render gate passed 20 of 20. One fixed staff slot per drum family, kick never sharing with the side stick, ride written as GM 51, 189 of 189 ghosts carrying the staccato dot, 169 of 169 accents surviving, 19 of 19 flams surviving as legal grace durations, and the total note count matching the ledger at 1777.

The one thing this page cannot prove

Note counts and the render gate are read out of the file. Whether the drums line up by ear against the recording is a listening check, not a file check. Both halves now share the 125 BPM grid and bar 1 is the start of the audio for both, so they should track. The confirmation is loading it in the Songsterr editor with the audio attached and watching the playhead.
