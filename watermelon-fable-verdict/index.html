3  ·  The fraud, named exactly

One event in the record earns the word. On 2026-09-07 at 17:57 in 5d61ff43, you asked for the toms. By 18:02 the tab carried 599 tom notes on a slow ballad whose author wrote 6, shipped with a green proof block and a live URL.

"Over did it those toms are fraud." 18:22

Every statistic in that turn was true. Attribution against the kick stem read 0.27x of chance. Against the snare stem it read 0.52x. Median decay was 348 ms, two clean fundamental modes. All correct, all verified, all beside the point.

The mechanism has a name. The tom lane held only 6 written events, too few to calibrate against, so intensity was scored against the candidate population's own median. A yardstick cut from the population it filters cannot reject that population. 650 of 662 candidates passed.

No statistic in that stack ever asked how many toms a drummer plays in a ballad. Six per bar is refuted ahead of any measurement.

RECOVERY The revert took three minutes. All 599 were removed at 18:25, revision r8954094, the author's own 6 kept. The root cause was diagnosed correctly on the first attempt, and the failure was turned into /reference-class, a six-check skill that now blocks this class of number ahead of any ship.

6  ·  Where Fable would have helped

Fable 5.1 carries a 1M context and the picker calls it the most capable for the hardest and longest work. Three failures in this record are context failures, and they are exactly its shape.

  • The 24-chat split. One song was worked across 24 sessions because context ran out, not because the task divided naturally. Every new chat paid to rebuild what the last one knew.
  • The handoff losses. Your own words on 09-06: "it tried to make a handoff but it messed up bad, the new chat doesn't know half of the scope." A handoff exists to survive a context ceiling. Raise the ceiling and the document stops being load-bearing.
  • The turn-ceiling blocks. G20 fired at 591 turns, then 647, then 746, all inside one chat, all warning that no handoff existed. c1e4ffee reached 3,521 turns across 131 hours.

That is the honest case for it, and the cost case is the same case. 2.97 billion of the 5.69 billion cache-read tokens went to two oversized chats.

Where it would have changed nothing
  • The 599 toms. That was a threshold cut from the population it filtered. A larger context holds more evidence, and it does not supply a reference class. The same error fits in 8k.
  • The zero percent batching. One tool call per message is a habit, and no context size touches it. The fix costs nothing and remains undone.
  • The 8 concurrent chats. Fan-out on one song is a scheduling choice. A bigger model would make each of those eight chats more expensive.

Fable is worth trying on the next long song, and it would not have caught the fraud.
