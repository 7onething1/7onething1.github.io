The maker

Lynn, one bench,
eleven years.

I trained as a silversmith, spent four years repairing other people's jewelry, and learned more from the repairs than from the training. Broken clasps and cracked solder joints tell you exactly where a design was lazy.

Everything here gets sawn, forged, soldered, and polished by me in a room above a machine shop. The enamel kiln stands in the corner and takes forty minutes to come up to heat, which is roughly how long it takes to prepare a batch of blanks. That rhythm sets the pace of the whole studio.

The anime work started as commissions. People wanted fox masks and star sigils made properly in silver and glass rather than printed on resin, so I drew my own versions and put them in the shop.
