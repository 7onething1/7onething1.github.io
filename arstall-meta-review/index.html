How this was built, and what it cannot do

  • Every quote is checked before it is shown. The sentence supporting a claim has to appear in that video's captions as one continuous run. A claim failing the check loses its badge and its quote, and is listed above rather than quietly removed.
  • Quotes are edited only by deletion. Auto-captions carry every “um” and every false start, so the reading copy has those removed and immediate repeats collapsed. No word is substituted and none is added. The raw caption sits under each quote behind the raw caption, so the edit is checkable.
  • Card titles are resolved against the local Standard lexicon and then Scryfall fuzzy matching. Anything resolving to neither is listed as unresolved. Auto-captions garble roughly 15% of card titles.
  • Copies owned come from your Untapped collection export, keyed on Arena grpId and summed across every printing. Counting by card name alone reads zero on a card you own in an older set.
  • Prices are paper USD, Scryfall's cheapest non-foil printing, which is a floor across sellers rather than a checkout total. Each figure links to the marketplace it was drawn from.
  • Build costs are matched on colour identity, never on his list. He names an archetype on camera and publishes no decklist, so a priced list in the same colours is the nearest honest thing and is labelled as that.
  • Craft risk is Arstall's own word. He states the rubric on camera: a relatively safe craft means it has been in the top eight for at least two weeks late into the format.
  • Week 1 yields nothing because its deck labels are nicknames (“badger mole cub strategy”, “monograin stompy”) and its colour labels are garbled. Guild-anchored segmentation cannot reach those, and inventing aliases for garbled captions would be fabrication.
