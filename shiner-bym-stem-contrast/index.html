1. The short answer

Multiple guitars: yes, on 8 of 10 songs. Multiple vocals: no, on any song, anywhere.

Eight songs carry two to four separate guitar stems. Not one song in any of the five BELIEVEYOUME roots I swept carries more than a single vocals file. The player flattens all 22 guitar stems down to 10 mono-ish guitar lanes, one per song.

The vocal question, settled

I swept five roots for any file whose name contains vocal, vox, backing, harmon, lead voc, choir, double, whisper, or scream:

  • T7 Shield/Moises_Stems/BelieveYouMeStems/: 10 hits, all named exactly vocals, one per song
  • T7 Shield/Stems/BELIEVEYOUME/: 0 hits
  • T7 Shield/BandBooks/BELIEVEYOUME/: 2 hits, both vocals.wav from a demucs 6-stem re-separation
  • T7 Shield/Music to Stem/Beleiveyoume/: 0 hits (source FLACs, un-stemmed)
  • Moises_Input/stems/htdemucs_6s/: 0 hits

No lead/backing split, no harmony stem, no doubles exist on disk today. Getting a multi-vocal player means re-separating with a model that splits vocals. That is a new render. No unused file is sitting there waiting.

3. What the player exposes

Source: drwu-htmls.vercel.app/believeyoume-stems, audio from drwu-stems.vercel.app/believeyoume-charts/stems/.

The page hard-codes six lanes for every song, with no per-song lane list:

const LANES = [
  {key:"drums",  label:"Drums"},
  {key:"bass",   label:"Bass"},
  {key:"guitar", label:"Guitar"},   <-- one lane; 22 disk stems fold into 10 of these
  {key:"piano",  label:"Keys"},
  {key:"vocals", label:"Vocals"},
  {key:"midi",   label:"Guide"}
];

I probed all 12 songs × 6 lanes on the live host with ranged GETs. Every lane returns 206 with content-type: audio/mpeg; a deliberate bogus path returns 404, so the probe distinguishes real files from a blanket fallback. All 70 expected lane files are present and real. The two midi gaps are declared in the page's own noLanes field, so they are honest.

4c. Fidelity

measured Disk stems are 44,100 Hz stereo WAV, roughly 44 MB per Mutiny stem. The site serves 96,021 bps CBR MP3, about 3.0 MB per lane. Equal-duration lanes therefore produce byte-identical file sizes. To rule out duplication I md5'd all six Mutiny lanes, and every one differs, so the lanes carry genuinely different audio. The house bar of 48 kHz / 24-bit applies to sample delivery, so 96 kbps counts as a deliberate streaming choice here.

4d. Two songs on the player are not on the album

measured The player's dropdown lists 12 songs. The album source folder Music to Stem/Beleiveyoume/BELIEVEYOUME (2025)/ holds exactly 10 FLACs. Number One Crush and Only Happy When It Rains are on the player, serve real audio for 5 lanes each, and have no stem folder in any BELIEVEYOUME root. inference Both titles are Garbage songs, so a covers session or a cross-project spill would explain it, and I have not confirmed which.

5. Four traps in the stem folders

Trap 1. The file called g3 looks like a third guitar and is close to empty

measured The Mutiny's g3.wav has mean −48.7 dBFS and peak −16.1 dBFS. Compare Rhythm Guitars at mean −17.3 / peak −0.0. That is roughly 31 dB below the real guitar and falls in the same range as the other drum residue stem (−47.6). Writing a third guitar part from this file means transcribing separation residue. It also correlates only +0.409 with the site's guitar lane, below other at +0.496.

Trap 2. The file called l is the kick drum

measured Mutiny's single-letter files abbreviate drum pieces. Band-energy fingerprint of l.wav: overall −19.4, below 120 Hz −20.7, 250 Hz–2.5 kHz −32.6, above 6 kHz −46.6. The So Far So Kick stem: −16.7 / −17.6 / −32.5 / −45.4. Same low-dominant shape within about 3 dB across all four bands. inference The letter l does not derive from "kick", so the abbreviation scheme is not simply first-initial and the label origin is unresolved. The audio is a kick regardless.

Trap 4. The stem map under-reports this album

measured ~/Projects/stem-map/stem-map.md lists BelieveYouMeStems as 7 songs with plain demucs role labels. The folder holds 10 songs and 101 files, and the Moises role names (Rhythm Guitars, Solo Guitars, RhythmGtr [L]) do not appear in the map at all. Anything that plans off the map alone will not know the multi-guitar stems exist. Regenerate with build_stem_map.py before trusting it for this album.

What the player gets right

All 70 expected lane files are live and real, verified against a 404 control. The two missing guide lanes are declared in the page's own noLanes field, so they do not fail silently. The buffer engine decodes every lane into a single AudioContext clock, so lanes cannot drift against each other. Missing lanes disable their own controls instead of playing silence under an active fader.

6. Method and provenance

  • measured File census: find over five roots, ._ resource forks excluded, roles parsed from filenames.
  • measured Levels: ffmpeg volumedetect, whole-file mean and peak, plus band splits at <120 Hz, 250 Hz–2.5 kHz, >6 kHz.
  • measured Source attribution: 20 ms RMS log-envelope, FFT cross-correlation with ±10 s lag search. Validated before use. A plain zero-lag sample correlation put the vocals control at r=−0.02 and was discarded as unusable; the envelope method put the same control at r=+0.847 as top match.
  • measured Live host: ranged GETs reading status, content-type and content-range; a bogus path returned 404 to prove the probe separates real files from a fallback.
  • inference Only three claims are inference and are tagged as such: the account for the two non-album songs, the origin of the l label, and the reading that g3 was meant as a guitar slot.
  • Per the stem-detection gate, a pitch found in one stem does not prove that instrument played it. Nothing here attributes an instrument to a part; this page reports which files exist, how loud they are, and which of them the player is serving.

If you want the multi-guitar detail on the player

The stems that would carry it exist today for 8 songs and need no new separation. The player needs a per-song lane list in place of the hard-coded six-lane array, since the lane sets genuinely differ (Mutiny has an L/R pair nothing else has, Not Too Much has strings, three songs have only lead/rhythm). Multi-vocal needs a re-separation first, because no file on disk supports it.
