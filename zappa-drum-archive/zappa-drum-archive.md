## 2. DRUM Magazine: `02-drummagazine/`

Ryan Brown's *Groove Analysis: Frank Zappa's Drummers* notation, at full resolution:
Who Are The Brain Police?, The Grand Wazoo, Zomby Woof, Andy, Muffin Man, Keep It Greasey, Drowning Witch.
Plus the 3 examples from the separate Colaiuta *Keep It Greasy* 19/16 lesson (7+5+7).
Article prose is in `12-article-text/`.

## 3. Pete Sweeney: `03-petesweeney/`

`Vinnietranscriptions.pdf`, 9 pages. **Verified page by page.** It is a Colaiuta bundle that happens to include two Zappa charts.
- p1–2, "All Blues" (Modern Drummer, April 1992, transcribed Jeff Wald), not Zappa
- p3–5, **"Dong Work For Yuda"**, transcribed Chris Brady (Rock Charts)
- p6+, **"Keep It Greasy"**, transcribed Marc Atkinson (Rock Charts, Modern Drummer Oct 2000)

## 4. Drumnet.ru: `06-drumnet/`

Free page scans for all six Zappa charts the site carries:
Oh No (3pp), Shut Up And Play Yer Guitar (6pp), Whipping Post (2pp), Muffin Man (4pp),
Watermelon In Easter Hay (5pp), Zomby Woof (1p).

**Correction to the source list:** the Zappa group page also links IDs 60, 757 and 868. Those are
The Cranberries "Zombie", Dolly Parton "9 to 5" and Michael Jackson "Billie Jean", site
cross-promotion. They were downloaded, identified by title, and moved to `delete/`.

## 5. Songsterr: `07-songsterr/`

The drum catalog is larger than either source list suggested: **50 Zappa drum tabs**, not the
6–17 named. Full index in `songsterr_zappa_drum_catalog.md` / `.json` with direct URLs.
Community transcriptions, check every hard passage against the recording and published notation.
