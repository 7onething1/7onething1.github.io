7. Steve Vai: Tempo Mental

29 notation examples covering nested tuplets, metric modulation, displaced rhythms and interrupted polyrhythms, the notation decisions Vai developed transcribing for Zappa, which is unusually direct evidence about how the hard rhythmic passages were heard and written down.

Corrections to the source list

ClaimWhat the files show
"Barry Wall's The Musical Worlds of Frank Zappa" The author is Ben Wall. Huddersfield MA thesis, 2011.
Drumnet supplies Zappa charts at the IDs on its group page Three of the nine linked IDs are cross-promotion: The Cranberries "Zombie", Dolly Parton "9 to 5", Michael Jackson "Billie Jean". Identified by title after download and set aside.
Pete Sweeney's PDF contains Dong Work for Yuda and Keep It Greasy True, from page 3 on. Pages 1–2 are a non-Zappa Colaiuta chart, so the file is a bundle rather than a Zappa pair.
Rondo Hatton essays download from the report index Ten of thirteen links point at a dead host that answers 200 with an HTML stub. Recovered from the afka.net mirror.
Songsterr covers roughly a dozen-plus drum parts 50 drum tabs.
zappa-analysis "contains more drum material than its main page suggests" Confirmed and quantified: 120 pages, 879 notation images.
