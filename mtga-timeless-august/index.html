Lands are most of the cost, and the button proves it. Leaving lands out, the median deck needs 14 rare or mythic wildcards and 4 of 37 fit a 10-wildcard budget. Counting the mana base, the median jumps to 22 and only 2 still fit. Both numbers are computed off the lists and stored; the toggle swaps which one is shown rather than deriving one from the other.
The rules.
  • Sideboards are out, by the creator's own split. 33 of 37 lists came from Moxfield's API with mainboard and sideboard as separate boards, so 422 sideboard cards are excluded because the creator filed them there, not because they fell past card 60. Companions live in their own board and are held out too, 8 of them. The 4 lists typed into a description carry no board data and keep the first-60 reading; they are badged inferred split on the page.
  • Remove lands is a toggle, on by default. Off, the mana base counts toward the wildcards. Rare lands are the largest single cost in Timeless, which is why both numbers ship rather than one.
  • Colors are read off required single-letter mana pips on nonland maindeck cards, then kept only where the deck plays a source that can make them. The pip rule alone is what the craft ranking uses and it broke here in a way specific to Timeless: a list titled Izzet Sneak and Show read WUBRG and a Simic Nadu list read WUBG, every extra colour coming from one card, Atraxa at {3}{G}{W}{U}{B}, which neither deck ever casts. Both cheat her into play. Against the 21 decks whose own name states a colour pair, the pip rule scored 67% and adding the castability check scored 81%. An any-colour source such as Chrome Mox is not counted as evidence, since Scryfall lists it as making all five while in play it makes exactly the one you imprinted. Colours set aside this way show as a small grey -WBG beside the pips. Read for 37 of 37 decks, 12 of them trimmed.
  • Different creators. Chestheir, Griffin MTG, Priority Holder, Takobyte. Every creator in the Standard corpus is excluded by name, so MTG Joe, CROKEYZ, d00mwake and LegenVD are all deliberately absent even though they post Timeless.
  • August 2026 only, and no rotation filter: Timeless is eternal, so a rotation limit here would be a check that always passes.
