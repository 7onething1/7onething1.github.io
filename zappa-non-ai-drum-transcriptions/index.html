What counts as a drum transcription here. A drum staff has to hold real notes, rests excluded. Songsterr classifies its own uploads with an aiGenerated flag, and every row below reads that flag off the meta API rather than guessing from the author name. A printed chart counts when the page carries drum-kit notation. A pitched score that happens to sit in a drum research folder does not count, and those are listed separately under what does not count.

Method

Every Songsterr figure comes from two public endpoints and no login: songsterr.com/api/meta/<songId> for the aiGenerated flag, author and revision id, then the CloudFront notation JSON per track for the note counts. Notes exclude rests. Drum tracks are the ones carrying instrumentId 1024.

Titles were searched at size=100. An earlier pass at size=30 hid the Andy tab s21495 behind ninety-nine unrelated results, so every title was re-listed at the larger cap before anything was recorded as missing. Album track order follows each record as released.

Printed sources were confirmed by opening the images, not by trusting the filenames. Byte sizes and md5 prefixes were taken on disk, which is how the duplicate filenames were caught: zomby-woof.png and GrooveAnalysis_zomby-woof.png are the same 40,126 bytes, and the counts above deduplicate them.
