# Zappa drum transcriptions besides the AI ones

Over-Nite Sensation, Roxy & Elsewhere, One Size Fits All, Joe's Garage. Measured 2026-09-05.

A drum staff has to hold real notes, rests excluded. Songsterr classifies its own uploads
with an `aiGenerated` flag, and every row reads that flag off the meta API rather than
guessing from the author name. A printed chart counts when the page carries drum-kit
notation. A pitched score sitting in a drum research folder does not count.

## What does not count

Kasper Sloots' zappa-analysis tree holds 1,175 files and is the best bar-and-meter map
available for all four records. The notation is pitched score on treble and bass clefs,
with no drum staff on the page. The PAS folder, the Steve Vai figures, the Rondo Hatton
editions and the two academic theses carry no drum charts either.

## Method

Every Songsterr figure comes from two public endpoints and no login: the meta API for
the `aiGenerated` flag, author and revision id, then the CloudFront notation JSON per
track for note counts. Notes exclude rests. Drum tracks carry `instrumentId 1024`.

Titles were searched at `size=100`. An earlier pass at `size=30` hid the Andy tab s21495
behind ninety-nine unrelated results, so every title was re-listed at the larger cap
before anything was recorded as missing.

Printed sources were confirmed by opening the images, not by trusting filenames. Byte
sizes and md5 prefixes were taken on disk, which caught the duplicate filenames.
