Two honesty notes on the numbers above

GarveeHome review counts are pooled. Both GarveeHome listings display 1,309 ratings, the identical number on two different rugs. That is a brand-level or parent-listing count spread across variations rather than 1,309 people rating that exact rug. The table says "pooled" instead of a count for that reason. Every other count on this page came off its own product page.

Nothing at $40 is a school carpet. Every rug here is a printed home rug doing classroom duty. Real circle-time carpet, meaning nylon face, bound reinforced edge, flame rating and marked seating spots, starts near $160 and runs past $300. That is worth knowing before you order.

The practical consequence: expect two or three years out of a $40 rug in daily classroom use, rather than the eight or ten a bound nylon carpet gives.

What to check before you order

Confirm the size in the cart. These listings sell 8 to 34 sizes under one page, and the size selector resets. The price jumps when you pick 8 × 10, so read it again at checkout.

Weight tells you thickness. At 8 × 10, anything under 10 lb is a thin printed sheet. The 16 and 17 lb options have real material in them.

Rubber over silicone dots. TPR or rubber backing grips waxed school tile. A printed dot grid slides once the floor is buffed.

Check the machine size. A 5 × 7 washes at home. An 8 × 10 usually needs a laundromat drum, so plan for that before the first juice spill.
