What to check on the listing before you order

Backing type. Rubber grips school tile. A printed silicone dot grid slides once the floor gets waxed.

Pile height. Low pile only. Anything shaggy traps glitter, sand and crumbs, and a classroom vacuum will not recover it.

Wash method. Machine washable matters more than stain resistant. Confirm the size fits a home machine, since a 9 × 12 usually needs a laundromat drum.

Review authenticity. Check whether the five-star reviews say "Vine Customer Review of Free Product". A rug with 8 ratings, half of them Vine, has almost no real signal behind it.
