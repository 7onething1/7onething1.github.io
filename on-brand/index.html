How work gets passed down, and what changes on the way

Artists absorb things and then change them. This site tracks what travelled between specific works, where it landed, and what was different when it arrived. Start anywhere and keep following the evidence.

This is a first vertical slice built from the project’s existing research documents, not a finished catalogue. Its point is that the whole path works end to end: a work, an episode, an exact line, an annotation, a typed connection, the other side’s exact line, an evidence packet, and the graph.
