The earlier side

S4E4 turns Andrea's efficiency review into a polygraph and then into unwanted intimacy with Lisa. Lisa says Andrea wants to be her friend and that this makes her nervous. Andrea calls them buddies, spares Lisa after learning her criminal history, confesses her own recent arson, asks Lisa to keep it secret, and asks for a hug.

The research describes this side rather than quoting it, so no exact script anchor is attached. The episode link above still resolves.

The later side

Britta arrives uninvited inside Frankie's car. Frankie asks why Britta chose her car out of the entire planet, then moves to sympathy, personal disclosure and an offer of help that same night.

Described rather than quoted in the source research.

What changes on the way

Andrea imposes the intimacy from above. Britta imposes it from below, and Frankie chooses to meet it. The direction of the intrusion reverses.

The changed element is part of the evidence rather than noise. A function can move to a different character, a power relation can reverse, several sources can combine, and the same mechanism can produce a different result.
