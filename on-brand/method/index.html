On Brand/Method

Method

Exact textual and structural recurrence is meaningful evidence even when no creator explains it publicly. Commentary, interviews, scripts, production records and credits can strengthen or confirm a connection. Their absence does not erase a visible textual relationship. This page says how the difference is recorded.

No confidence scores

A percentage implies a measurement nobody took. Each record instead carries a research state grounded in what the source situation supports, and the wording the original research document used is preserved on the record rather than being paraphrased into a house style.

Standing principles

  • A missing public quote does not disprove intentionality, and resemblance alone does not establish it.
  • Failure to locate a source in a quick web search is not evidence that the source or claim does not exist. When the project already identifies an interview, commentary track, script, transcript, file, date, publication, or remembered quotation, that route must be checked directly before the claim can be downgraded.
  • Duplicate mentions are not extra votes. Several documents repeating one earlier note is one piece of evidence, not several.

What each kind of evidence can carry

An evidence item has to say what it actually proves. This is the difference between a packet a reader can inspect and a summary a reader has to trust.

KindWhat it proves
Primary textThat the wording, action, or staging exists in the work. It does not establish provenance.
Creator statementWhat the creator said about method or intent on the record. A statement about method does not confirm one particular reading.
Production recordCredits, personnel, studio, or scheduling. Establishes access or overlap, never conscious borrowing.
Casting recordWho appeared, and in what prior role. Persona intent needs its own evidence.
Contemporaneous receptionThat an audience recognised something at the time. It does not prove authorial intent.
Commentary trackWhat the participants said while reviewing their own work.
Archival materialThat a document or post existed on a date.
Scholarship or reference workAn established external fact, such as a term's documented history.
Project synthesisNothing on its own. It is the argument that the other items form a pattern, and it is labelled as ours.

Chronology is a hard constraint

An earlier work can inform a later one. A later work cannot become the historical source of an earlier one. Later material can still create a retrospective echo, an actor-history layer, a callback or a reinterpretation, and those are separate relationship types with separate rules. The validator refuses to build the site if an ancestry edge runs backwards.

Direction classRule
ancestrySource must predate target. Violation is a hard validation error.
internalSource and target must belong to the same work. Source should precede target.

retrospectiveTarget postdates source; meaning is created after the source aired. Never evidence of authorial foresight.

undirectedNo temporal claim is made.

Amalgams, not copies

A character can inherit one behaviour from one predecessor, a relationship function from another, a scene structure from a film, and a meta layer from an actor’s earlier work. The active ancestor can change by episode or by pairing. The site therefore keeps role mappings local to the comparison that produced them, rather than flattening the research into permanent one-to-one equivalents.

You can see this working on the takeover mirror, where the truth-teller function sits with Abed even though the project’s larger map sends that show’s equivalent character elsewhere.

Rights

Full transcripts are held privately so research keeps its exact anchors. Public pages show the passages an argument rests on, with the speaker where a source proves one, a locator, and surrounding context. Republishing a complete copyrighted script is a different act from quoting the lines under analysis, and the data model keeps those apart.

Evidentiary images are real or absent. No AI-generated stand-ins for actors, scenes, musicians, historical objects or production stills. Where rights are uncertain, a sourced outbound card appears instead of a fabricated substitute.

On Brand is a research map. Every claim on it carries a research state and a route back to its evidence. States are the project’s own categories, not confidence percentages: see how the evidence is graded.
