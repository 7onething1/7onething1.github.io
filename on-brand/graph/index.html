Graph

Not a force-directed cloud where everything floats. Time runs left to right and each work keeps its own band, so an object’s position tells you what kind of relationship you are looking at. An arrow pointing left would be an impossible ancestry claim, which is why none appear: the validator refuses to build one.

30 units and 34 edges, 1996 to 2022. At this size the whole neighbourhood fits in one view. As the corpus grows the page will load a neighbourhood around the selected object and expand on demand, rather than loading the whole database.
