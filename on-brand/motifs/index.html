Motifs

Some research does not belong under a single episode pair. A motif is a recurring mechanism with its own chronology, and the names here are the ones the research already uses.

  • Institutional takeover and replacement

    NewsRadioCommunity

    A crisis opens a legitimate doorway, the rightful leader is removed, a governing body supplies legitimacy, the correct skeptic is disbelieved, and the regime's own machinery supplies the reversal.

  • Repairman mythology

    NewsRadioCommunity

    Practical repair competence is treated as beneath a character, then turns out to define him. Joe Garrelli is WNYX's electrician; Troy Barnes is eventually named the Truest Repairman.

  • Managed choice and induced agency

    NewsRadioCommunity

    A controller arranges circumstances so the target experiences a directed outcome as a personal choice, preserving the target's sense of agency.

  • Secret-exposure machinery

    NewsRadioCommunity

    Concealed private behaviour becomes the engine of ensemble conflict, through anonymous complaints, polygraphs, found material, cameras, or leaked email.

  • Performed identity and demystification

    NewsRadioCommunity

    Attraction attaches to a performed persona and collapses when the mechanics are explained.

  • Magical token separated from its magic

    NewsRadioCommunity

    An ordinary coin participates in a ritual while the text explicitly denies that the object contains the power.

  • Guest-star reversibility

    NewsRadioCommunity

    A one-episode guest is given an exit route that preserves the continuing character's canon rather than overwriting it.

  • Hairpin

    evermoreMidnightsThe Hairpin Drop Heard Around the World

    An ordinary idiom is altered so that the word “hairpin” survives, in a vocabulary with a documented queer history.

  • Balloon and around-the-world recursion

    NewsRadioCommunity

    Balloon travel and global voyage imagery recur, from Jimmy James's fake balloon trip to Troy's eventual voyage.

On Brand is a research map. Every claim on it carries a research state and a route back to its evidence. States are the project’s own categories, not confidence percentages: see how the evidence is graded.
