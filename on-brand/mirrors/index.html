Mirrors

A mirror is not two episode names and a paragraph saying they resemble each other. It is a sequence of aligned beats, each with its own evidence, its own state, and a statement of what changed.

The shared packet is unusually dense: emergency access, long-planned opportunism, physical removal of the rightful authority, disbelief directed at the correct skeptic, control of the decisive evidence, board-assisted legitimacy, the institution as symbolic home, an internal resistance movement, and a reversal that runs through the regime's own machinery.

On Brand is a research map. Every claim on it carries a research state and a route back to its evidence. States are the project’s own categories, not confidence percentages: see how the evidence is graded.
