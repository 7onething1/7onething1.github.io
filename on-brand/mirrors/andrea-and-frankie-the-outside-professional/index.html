Andrea and Frankie: the outside professional who reorganises everyone

A competent outsider arrives with real authority, reorganises the relationship map, and changes what counts as a private matter inside the group. The arrival is closely parallel. What happens to privacy afterwards is where the two shows diverge most sharply.

The headline changeAndrea treats friendship as entitling her to know. Frankie treats her own life as nobody's business and gives the group nothing. The same privacy machinery runs in reverse.

Who performs which function, inside this comparison

These mappings are local to this mirror. A character can perform one function here and a different one elsewhere, and the project’s larger map may send the same character down another line entirely. That is the model working, not a contradiction.
