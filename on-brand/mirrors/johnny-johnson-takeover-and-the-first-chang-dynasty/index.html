Johnny Johnson's takeover and Chang's First Dynasty

One corporate succession machine and one campus coup, aligned beat by beat. The shared packet is unusually dense: emergency access, long-planned opportunism, physical removal of the rightful authority, disbelief directed at the correct skeptic, control of the decisive evidence, board-assisted legitimacy, the institution as symbolic home, an internal resistance movement, and a reversal that runs through the regime's own machinery.
