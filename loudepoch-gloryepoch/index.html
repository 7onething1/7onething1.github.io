Two original VST3 plug-ins, one verification report

LoudEpoch
and GloryEpoch

LoudEpoch is a guitar rig plug-in, modeled by black-box measurement of a commercial rig plug-in, the JHS "Loud Is More Good" as rendered by MixWave. GloryEpoch is an overdrive modeled on the JHS Morning Glory through MixWave's plug-in. Both are original code. Every figure on this page is copied from the verification files of the two projects, and each section names its source file.

1. Measured signal flow

The routing was proven by bit-exact cascade tests: render two modules together, then render each alone and feed one through the other. The true order reproduces the combined render at -240 dB error, wrong orders land 1 to 50 dB off.

Source: LoudEpoch/docs/ROUTING.md (order and proofs), docs/INTEGRATION.md (latency table).

2. Modules at a glance

Color rule is an editorial call on the numbers in each card. Green: every stated check passes or headline error is within about 1 dB. Amber: headline error is usable but the file lists real gaps. Red: the module missed its own stated accuracy target.

Charts plot the headline metric named in each bar from VERIFICATION.md. Metrics differ per module, so compare within a bar pair, not across modules. Delay percent is 100 of 101 and Reverb 136 of 136 metrics within tolerance.

Studio blocks

close

What it does. Gate, input and output levels, EQs, studio compressors, output limiter.

How measured. Routing proven by bit-exact cascade tests; filter and dynamics values fitted to the reference audio. Gate threshold sweeps used for gate placement.

Headline A/B. All 24 test groups PASS their stated limits: level law 0.000 dB, comp static max 0.001 dB, comp dynamics rms 0.50 / max 2.5 dB, limiter static max 0.022 dB, EQ holdout rms 0.01 to 0.06 dB.

Known gaps. Gate soft-open law rms 1.08 / max 4.53 dB near the opening level; gate burst envelope rms 2.0 / max 4.9 dB. Filter tables measured at 48 kHz and applied unchanged at other rates. No real-audio program test.

Source: VERIFICATION.md, docs/ROUTING.md, tests/modules/studio/ab_table.md

Compressor pedal

usable, gaps

What it does. Feed-forward detector, soft knee, tilt EQ, Dirt clipper.

How measured. Topology from a published form with measured fits; 22 scenarios.

Headline A/B. 1 kHz static curve rms mean 0.64 dB (median 0.15); 100 Hz static rms mean 0.62 dB; third-octave below 9 kHz at -20 dBFS rms mean 0.72 dB. Sine gain error max 5.0 dB (Comp 7.5/10 latch). Burst gain-curve rms median 1.73 dB, worst 8.89 dB (Comp 10). THD error max mean 5.5 points.

Known gaps. Attack latch at Comp 7.5 and 10 is not modeled. The cold-start latch at Comp 5.111 is modeled but fitted at 1 kHz only. Dirt-off H3 error median 11 dB (max 36). The standalone tables were not re-run after the latch change.

Source: VERIFICATION.md, tests/modules/comp/ab_table.md

Overdrive

missed targets

What it does. Morning Glory style op-amp and FET model, copied verbatim from the GloryEpoch engine.

How measured. Topology, calibrated. Numbers are supplied by the GloryEpoch refit owner and were not re-measured in LoudEpoch.

Headline A/B. 1 kHz gain error mean 2.89 / max 8.34 dB; third-octave rms mean 2.31 / worst 3.14 dB; THD error mean 5.5 dB.

Known gaps. Missed the pass-2 accuracy targets (see the GloryEpoch section). Hi-Cut effect present but oversized at Drive 0. Only an on/off oversampling choice.

Source: VERIFICATION.md, docs/INTEGRATION.md, GloryEpoch/VERIFICATION.md

Distortion

usable, gaps

What it does. Drive, symmetric clipper, tone stack.

How measured. Topology with measured fits; 33 scenarios.

Headline A/B. 1 kHz static rms mean 0.87 / max 1.28 dB; 100 Hz static rms mean 1.68 / max 3.32 dB; third-octave below 9 kHz rms about 1.1 dB (max 2.56); H3 error median 6.0 / max 7.9 dB; THD error median 7.1 points (max 72, a mix-5 null outlier).

Known gaps. The reference has weak even harmonics that the symmetric clipper does not produce, so H3 and THD stay off.

Source: VERIFICATION.md, tests/modules/dist/ab_table.md

Amp

usable, gaps

What it does. Yeh and Smith tone stack, two clipper stages.

How measured. Topology with measured fits at 32 corners.

Headline A/B. Fundamental gain error rms mean 0.77 dB (max 2.58, worst single 3.95); THD error rms mean 2.66 dB (max 9.3); H2 rms mean 5.7 dB; H3 rms mean 2.4 dB; third-octave rms mean 1.96 dB.

Known gaps. Third-octave max 19.7 dB at the all-minimum tone corner start-up artifact. At 100 Hz under heavy saturation the reference pulse trains reach 55 to 70 percent THD and the model 25 to 55.

Source: VERIFICATION.md, tests/modules/amp/ab_table.md

Cab

usable, gaps

What it does. 21 mics across 4 cabinets as our own fitted biquad cascades. No impulse responses are shipped.

How measured. Measured, 21 mics x 4 cabinets.

Headline A/B. Third-octave core error rms: grid groups mean 0.47 dB; mic and cab matrix 0.25 to 1.12 dB with single bands up to 5.5 dB; multi-slot groups mean about 1.1 dB; misc max 3.74 dB.

Known gaps. Distance for pairs far from the fitted corrections reaches 3 to 4 dB rms. Two slots with opposite polarity are cancellation-sensitive (2.6 dB rms). The reference path has about 38 samples of latency that ours does not model.

Source: VERIFICATION.md, tests/modules/cab/ab_table.md

Delay

close

What it does. Delay line, FIR darkening, soft-limited feedback, tilt shelf.

How measured. Topology with measured fits, 101 metrics.

Headline A/B. 100 of 101 metrics within tolerance. Modulation depth and rate match to about 1 percent.

Known gaps. The one failing metric is feedback compression at fb 7.5 / -6 dB (ratio 1.85 against 0.95). Mod Intensity and Speed laws are a power law through one measured mid point.

Source: VERIFICATION.md, tests/modules/delay/out/ab_table.md

Reverb

close

What it does. 8-line feedback delay network.

How measured. Topology with measured decay, level and tone fits, 136 metrics.

Headline A/B. 136 of 136 metrics within tolerance. RT60 within 0.4 s at 5 frequencies for 5 decay settings; level within 1.5 dB.

Known gaps. None listed in the verification file beyond the shared sample-rate caveat.

Source: VERIFICATION.md, tests/modules/reverb/out/ab_table.md

The 0 dBFS level bug

The first integration run showed the chain 14 to 28 dB low against the reference at 0 dBFS. Level isolation found the Compressor pedal as the first module to diverge (REF -0.99 dBFS, ours -9.60). The reference has a cold-start latch: an abrupt onset at or above about -1.4 dBFS leaves gain reduction disengaged until the level falls under about -1.9 dBFS. Our compressor always compressed, so everything downstream saw 8.6 dB too little signal. The fix models the latch; the latched output now matches within 0.10 dB at 0 dBFS.

What remains. The latch now over-triggers at 2 kHz (Default 2 kHz at 0 dBFS reads -0.14 against REF -7.68), Pop GT Delay at 1 kHz is 4.9 dB low, and the latch thresholds were fitted at Comp 5.111 only. Crunchy Rhythm and Hot Lead do not use the Compressor pedal and are unchanged. This is a model of the reference, not a match to it.

6. Not verified

  • No listening test of any kind for either plug-in; no audio was played.
  • No load in Ableton Live 11 (LoudEpoch: not tried). GloryEpoch was not opened in Live and Live 11.3.30 compatibility is not checked.
  • Steinberg VST3 validator not run for either build; only pluginval at strictness 10 and the JUCE host test.
  • Host-supplied tempo in a real DAW (tested only through the BPM parameter in the host test).
  • Latency change while playing in a real DAW.
  • arm64 and Windows builds: Intel x86_64 only.
  • Real-time CPU load was not measured.
  • GloryEpoch pass-2 accuracy targets were not met (1 kHz gain error and third-octave sweep rms).
  • GloryEpoch fit ran at 1x oversampling, not 4x; no 4x refit of the final set. Three corners were left out of the final joint fit.
  • GloryEpoch Mix law is one probe (Mix 5), 6.7 dB off at -24 dBFS; filter shapes measured at one frequency each, dry path only.
  • GloryEpoch fitted constants are behavioral values, not recovered component values; the component positions are inferred, not from a published schematic.
  • LoudEpoch full chain: Default 2 kHz at 0 dBFS is +7.54 dB high and Pop GT Delay 1 kHz is 4.9 dB low; THD differences range from -16 to about +40 points; noise third-octave error reaches 11.5 dB in single bands.
  • LoudEpoch standalone Compressor A/B tables were not re-run after the latch change. Not probed on the reference: Input Level other than 0, Input Type STEREO, Oversampling OFF/2X/4X, sag, and real-audio program material.

7. Provenance

No reference audio, impulse response, wavetable or coefficient table of the reference is in LoudEpoch's Source folder or its bundle. A name check of the source and the installed bundle for the reference or vendor names found no matches. Reference numbers come from audio rendered through the reference plug-in, and the reference was never disassembled or dumped. Cab responses are our own fitted biquad cascades and no impulse responses ship. For GloryEpoch, reading the factory preset XML for the control list and test corners is the only use of preset data, and nothing from it is shipped.

The LoudEpoch Overdrive module is a verbatim copy of GloryEpoch's engine files, copied 2026-11-23. GloryEngine.cpp sha256 1ce59862c19e963b00184d7752b6e5f3279f28c79367759c2d13fb3381d97f7a, GloryEngine.h sha256 b7047c6c35eaf20840e754bee51d64cacffb761d61db2fa2441b6efa7d359e6c.
