A chat that goes quiet and the babysitter going quiet produce the same signature. Neither one raises anything, and both look like calm.

Fix 1 idle ladder

The dedup key was uuid : rules : turns/500. A dead chat's turn count never grows, so its key froze the moment it stopped working. The key now carries an idle step, crossing at 1h 4h 12h 1d 3d 7d, so a parked chat returns to the alarm on a widening schedule instead of once.

Result on the first sweep after the change: 28 chats re-armed, including one at 20.3 days idle that had been frozen out of the alarm entirely.

Machine sleep is suppressed by comparison. The watchdog records its own last run. When the babysitter's gap and the watchdog's gap match within 3 minutes, both clocks paused together, so the Mac slept and nothing is broken. A gap on the babysitter alone still alarms.

Who watches the watchdog. Each one asserts the other. The babysitter pushes WATCHDOG DOWN when the watchdog heartbeat passes 45 minutes, and the watchdog pushes on any of the seven above. Two independent launchd jobs on different intervals, so one dying is caught by the other, and both dying at once shows as two stale heartbeats at the top of this page.
