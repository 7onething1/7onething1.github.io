The question

Songsterr's Zappa tabs are community and AI made. The archive built on 28 August holds published notation for these same two records, so each tab can be checked against a source with real editorial standing rather than taken on trust. One song per pass. This is pass 1, Zomby Woof, chosen because it has two independent published sources, three tabs to compare, and the odd-meter writing that makes a transcription easy to get wrong.

What the published side actually says

Two independent sources. Their overlap is limited, so what they give is combined coverage without contradiction rather than mutual corroboration. Sloots documents one passage, Brown documents a different one, and neither confirms the other's bars.

1. Kasper Sloots / Paul Pappas, zappa-analysis.com

Full score of Zomby Woof from 1:39 to 2:10, updated fall 2024, deposited at the I-depot in The Hague. He identifies it as a transcription from the recording. A separate interlude image on the same page is reproduced from the Over-Nite Sensation Guitar Book, page 64, serving as a comparison rather than as the source of the excerpt. He prints thirteen bars, running 7/8, 5/4, 10/16 ×4, 7/8, 6/4, 4/4 ×5.

2. Ryan Brown, DRUM Magazine

"It has lots of fives, including groups of five sixteenth-notes and quintuplets. The song also has a lot of different time signatures."

"I always hear the 15/16 section as two groups of notes: one group of four eighth-notes, plus one group of seven sixteenth-notes."

Four eighths is 8/16, plus seven sixteenths is 15/16. The arithmetic checks.

Combined published signature labels for Zomby Woof: 4/4, 5/4, 6/4, 7/8, 10/16, 15/16. Six labels drawn from two sources covering different passages. No label here is confirmed by both.

One of the three tabs uses all six published signature labels. The community hand tab carries every one, and its bars 38 to 50 run 7/8, 5/4, 10/16 ×4, 7/8, 6/4, 4/4 ×5, matching the thirteen bars Sloots prints, in order. That makes it the strongest provisional bar map. A label matching a label is not yet a verified transcription.
The AI tab flattens the song. 119 of its 123 bars are 4/4. It has none of 5/4, 10/16 or 15/16, and it carries a 13/16 and a 9/8 that neither published source shows. Its drum track holds 297 notes against the hand tab's 1,612, and 115 of those carry a null pitch, so the part is malformed as well as thin.
Yours is the densest drum candidate with the closest calculated duration. 2,076 drum notes shows density. 5:08.23 against the album's 5:11 shows approximate structural coverage. Zero parenthesised notes shows compliance with the house file rule. None of those three measurements establishes accurate drums, appropriate cymbals, playable orchestration, or correct attack placement, so it stays a detailed repair candidate pending comparison against the audio. It writes at 175 bpm in 232 bars, double-time against 88 bpm in 114, a legitimate choice. What it does not carry is the 7/8, 10/16 or 15/16 labelling, with twelve bars of 5/8 in their place.

The alignment test

A matching list of labels proves less than it looks like, since the same labels could sit in the wrong bars. Sloots states his own timestamps and prints thirteen bars, so the tab's tempo map gives a check on both the span and its contents.

Correction, 29 August. A first version of this page mapped nine bars and reported the alignment as 2 percent. That figure came from dividing each endpoint offset by its absolute position in the song, which measures nothing about alignment. Across the excerpt itself the nine-bar span is 31.2 percent short. The fault was mapping nine bars against the thirteen Sloots prints.

Thirteen bars is both the count Sloots prints and the closest duration match, at 32.00 s against 31.0 s. Its endpoints sit at +2.07 s and +3.07 s, so the tab runs about one second long across the passage. The thirteen-bar run is 7/8, 5/4, 10/16, 10/16, 10/16, 10/16, 7/8, 6/4, 4/4, 4/4, 4/4, 4/4, 4/4, which is the order and the page breaks Sloots prints.

Method

Songsterr notation comes from the public meta API and its CloudFront path, with no browser and no login, which is the route recorded in the songsterr-tab-guide skill. Published notation comes from the Zappa drum archive built 28 August. Meters, tempo maps and note counts are read from the tab JSON directly. Nothing here is read off a rendered screenshot.

Two repairs were needed to the archive during this pass. Its Zomby Woof page had been saved as a Cloudflare interstitial rather than content, and 21 other pages had the same problem at the tail of the crawl, so 13 real pages were refetched. Separately, the first crawl kept images only from pages that mentioned drums, which left most per-song notation for these two records missing: 113 further images were fetched and validated.
