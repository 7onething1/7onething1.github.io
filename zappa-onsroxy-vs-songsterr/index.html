The question

Songsterr's Zappa tabs are community and AI made. The archive built on 28 August holds published notation for these same two records, so each tab can be checked against a source with real editorial standing rather than taken on trust. One song per pass. This is pass 1, Zomby Woof, chosen because it has two independent published sources, three tabs to compare, and the odd-meter writing that makes a transcription easy to get wrong.

What the published side actually says

Two independent sources, neither derived from the other.

1. Kasper Sloots / Paul Pappas, zappa-analysis.com

Full score of Zomby Woof from 1:39 to 2:10, updated fall 2024, deposited at the I-depot in The Hague. Its interlude page cites the official Over-Nite Sensation Guitar Book, page 64. The notated meter sequence runs 7/8, 5/4, 10/16, 10/16, then 7/8, 6/4, 4/4.

2. Ryan Brown, DRUM Magazine

"It has lots of fives, including groups of five sixteenth-notes and quintuplets. The song also has a lot of different time signatures."

"I always hear the 15/16 section as two groups of notes: one group of four eighth-notes, plus one group of seven sixteenth-notes."

Four eighths is 8/16, plus seven sixteenths is 15/16. The arithmetic checks.

Combined published meter set for Zomby Woof: 4/4, 5/4, 6/4, 7/8, 10/16, 15/16. Six meters, from two sources that agree.

The community hand tab is the only one that reproduces the published meter map. It carries all six, and its bars 38 to 46 run 7/8, 5/4, 10/16 ×4, 7/8, 6/4, 4/4, which is the Sloots sequence in order.
The AI tab flattens the song. 119 of its 123 bars are 4/4. It has none of 5/4, 10/16 or 15/16, and it invents a 13/16 and a 9/8 that neither published source shows. Its drum track holds 297 notes against the hand tab's 1,612, and 115 of those carry a null pitch, so the part is malformed as well as thin.
Your tab is the most detailed and the closest in length, and it writes the odd meters straight. 2,076 drum notes, 5:08.23 against the album's 5:11, and zero parenthesised notes, so it already meets the house standard the other two break. It writes at 175 bpm in 232 bars, double-time against the hand tab's 88 bpm in 114, which is a legitimate choice. What it loses is the meter writing: no 7/8, no 10/16, no 15/16, with twelve bars of 5/8 in their place.

Method

Songsterr notation comes from the public meta API and its CloudFront path, with no browser and no login, which is the route recorded in the songsterr-tab-guide skill. Published notation comes from the Zappa drum archive built 28 August. Meters, tempo maps and note counts are read from the tab JSON directly. Nothing here is read off a rendered screenshot.

Two repairs were needed to the archive during this pass. Its Zomby Woof page had been saved as a Cloudflare interstitial rather than content, and 21 other pages had the same problem at the tail of the crawl, so 13 real pages were refetched. Separately, the first crawl kept images only from pages that mentioned drums, which left most per-song notation for these two records missing: 113 further images were fetched and validated.
