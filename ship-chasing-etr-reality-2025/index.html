These sources are not playing the same game

Establish The Run sells projections and rankings, so the honest way to grade them is ranking accuracy, and there is an independent referee for that: FantasyPros freezes every expert's ranks and scores them against the final box score.

Ship Chasing and Davis Mattek are best-ball tournament players. Their product is a drafted roster in a large-field contest, so the honest way to grade them is tournament results and the specific players they staked a position on. Each side gets its own scoreboard below.

02

The best-ball scoreboard

Underdog Best Ball Mania VI

Ship Chasing's whole ethos is the large-field best-ball tournament. The cleanest reality test is simple: did their approach win the biggest one? In the 2025 contest, it did.

The $2,000,000 result

Best Ball Mania VI drew more than 670,000 entries and narrowed to a 539-person Week 17 final. The top prize of $2 million went to Sam Sherman, who holds roles across Ship Chasing, Establish The Run, and Legendary Upside.

The winning build was a textbook Hero-RB, and the early core is exactly what these shows preach, one anchor back plus a stack of receivers:

2.04 Derrick Henry (Hero RB)  +  WR wall: Jaxon Smith-Njigba · Amon-Ra St. Brown · Tetairoa McMillan · Chris Olave

Reality backed the build. In final 2025 PPR scoring, Henry finished RB8, Smith-Njigba WR2, and Amon-Ra St. Brown WR3. The pieces the roster was built around all hit.

Mattek's claim, labeled as a claim

Davis Mattek posted that "33% of the Best Ball Mania winners have been ship chasing hosts." That is his own public statement, not an audit run here. What is independently verified is the single data point above: a Ship Chasing affiliate took down the 2025 edition for two million dollars.

The reading

What reality actually says

ETR

Backed by the referee. On the one contest that measures them, in-season ranking accuracy, ETR finished second of more than 159 experts and has now placed top-4 four years running. Their reputation for projections survives contact with the box score.

Ship Chasing

Backed by the money. A Ship Chasing affiliate won the $2M Best Ball Mania VI on the exact Hero-RB and receiver-wall shape the shows preach, and the roster's early core finished RB8, WR2, and WR3. The tournament result is real, the "33% of winners" line stays labeled as Mattek's own claim.

The calls

The wins came from the tails. Kerrane's differentiated 2025 bets ran even at 4-4, and that is the job in best ball. Pickens to WR5 and McBride to TE1 out of Round 5 are the kind of hits that win leagues, and most of the misses came from injuries. The market, drafting against him, proved no safer, missing the Etienne bust outright.

Method, scoring, and honesty notes

Every finish on this page is a full-PPR 2025 regular-season total pulled from StatMuse and confirmed to be the 2025 season. Underdog best ball is half-PPR, so exact position ranks shift a little from the full-PPR ranks shown, the gaps here are wide enough that no verdict turns on the scoring format.

The Section 4 sleepers and busts are ESPN and CBS preseason consensus, presented as the market Ship Chasing drafted against. They are not attributed to ETR, Ship Chasing, or Davis Mattek.

What could not be verified is left out. A secondhand line that Pat Kerrane had also won a Best Ball Mania could not be confirmed, so it is not claimed. Mattek's "33%" is his own post, treated as a claim, not an audited figure.
