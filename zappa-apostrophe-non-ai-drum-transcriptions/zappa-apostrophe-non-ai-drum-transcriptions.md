# Apostrophe: drum transcriptions besides the AI ones

Frank Zappa, DiscReet 1974. Measured 2026-09-05.

Apostrophe is the best-covered Zappa record on this Mac. Eight of the nine tracks carry a
human drum reading, and the ninth is the one with a printed chart. Compare Roxy & Elsewhere,
where two tracks of ten carry a human tab and no printed chart exists at all.

A drum staff has to hold real notes, rests excluded. Songsterr classifies its own uploads
with an `aiGenerated` flag, and every row reads that flag off the meta API rather than
guessing from the author name. A printed chart counts when the page carries drum-kit
notation, confirmed by opening the image.

## The two printed charts

Both come from Todd Bishop, published across cruiseshipdrummer.com and pdxdrummer.com, and
both were confirmed by opening the image rather than by reading the filename.

**Don't Eat the Yellow Snow, drums by Ralph Humphrey.** A full page with a seven-line
legend: crash, hihat, high tom, snare, low tom, bass, hihat/foot. Marked Transcription
begins at 0:32. Barred 7/8 against 7/4 through the opening, dropping to common time at the
close. Bar numbers run to 33 and the last system reads Segue to Nanook Rubs It. The JPEG is
261,313 bytes at 1236x1600 and the PDF is one page. Copyright 2013 Todd Bishop.

**Apostrophe', drums by Jim Gordon.** Headed Apostrophe, drum break. Marked Drum break
begins at 3:22. Two systems, carrying (rim) on the first and (china) on the second. The
JPEG is 40,785 bytes at 612x792 and the PDF is one page. Copyright 2011 Todd Bishop. It is
an excerpt of the break, and it is the only drum notation of the studio take on hand.

Two of the nine tracks are ours on Songsterr. Nanook Rubs It s35865 carries 1,309 drum

here and excluded from the transcription counts.

## What does not count

Kasper Sloots' zappa-analysis pages for this record are pitched score on treble and bass
clefs with no drum staff, verified by opening the notation. Uncle Meat (YCDTOSA Vol. 2)
s20690 is a search false positive on the Uncle Remus query and belongs to a different
record. Don't Eat The Yellow Snow (Ukulele) s2561609 carries no drum track.

## Method

Every Songsterr figure comes from two public endpoints and no login: the meta API for the
`aiGenerated` flag, author and revision id, then the CloudFront notation JSON per track for
note counts. Notes exclude rests. Drum tracks carry `instrumentId 1024`.

Twelve search patterns were run at `size=100`, covering the nine album titles plus the short
forms Yellow Snow, Nanook and Stinkfoot, since a `size=30` cap on an earlier sweep of
another record hid a real tab behind ninety-nine unrelated results. Nineteen Zappa songIds
came back and seventeen carry a drum track.

All eighteen in-scope tabs were already in Brandon's Songsterr favorites when this was
built, checked against `/api/favorites` with nothing missing.
