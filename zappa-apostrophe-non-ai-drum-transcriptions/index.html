Apostrophe is the best-covered Zappa record on this Mac. Eight of the nine tracks carry a human drum reading, and the ninth is the one with a printed chart. Compare Roxy & Elsewhere, where two tracks of ten carry a human tab and no printed chart exists at all.
What counts as a drum transcription here. A drum staff has to hold real notes, rests excluded. Songsterr classifies its own uploads with an aiGenerated flag, and every row reads that flag off the meta API rather than guessing from the author name. A printed chart counts when the page carries drum-kit notation, confirmed by opening the image.

Method

Every Songsterr figure comes from two public endpoints and no login: songsterr.com/api/meta/<songId> for the aiGenerated flag, author and revision id, then the CloudFront notation JSON per track for note counts. Notes exclude rests. Drum tracks carry instrumentId 1024.

Twelve search patterns were run at size=100, covering the nine album titles plus the short forms Yellow Snow, Nanook and Stinkfoot, since a size=30 cap on an earlier sweep of another record hid a real tab behind ninety-nine unrelated results. Nineteen Zappa songIds came back and seventeen carry a drum track.

All eighteen in-scope tabs were already in Brandon's Songsterr favorites when this was built, checked against /api/favorites with nothing missing.
