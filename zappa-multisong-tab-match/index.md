## Corrections made during this pass

- The refractory explanation for Keep It Greasey was wrong. Kick-lane gaps under 30 ms are
  4.4% (57 of 1,310), not the 27.8% I first reported,
  which counted cross-lane gaps that the detector never merges.
- The tempo-boundary explanation was also wrong. Zero-confirm and confirming bars sit near a
  tempo change at the same 12% rate.
- The guess that My Mirror Hates Me had a hat part written into the tom lane was refuted.
  MIDI 41 reaches 1.25x chance on toms and 1.19x on hat, and nothing else in the kit supports it.
- Shiner tempos were first estimated by search, then replaced with the real values from
  part["automations"]["tempo"]. Several verdicts moved.

14 of 30 Shiner stem FLACs carry an unset total-samples header and decode short. Originals untouched;
