The rules, and what each one means in the data.
  • 10 rare or mythic crafts. Counted as copies in the deck minus copies you own. A card you already have a playset of costs nothing.
  • Uncommon and common are unlimited, so they never block a deck. The column is there anyway, because unlimited is a budget and not a promise the number is small.
  • Lands do not count, toward either the wildcards or the rotation limit. Rare lands are the largest wildcard cost in Standard, so this changes the answer a lot.
  • Sideboards do not count. Maindeck is the first 60 cards of each harvested list. The board split was discarded at harvest, so that boundary is an inference. Across the corpus 140 of 253 lists past 60 cards have exactly 15 past the line, and basic lands sit 1,668 inside it against 92 outside.
  • At most 4 rotating cards, read as 4 distinct card names that leave Standard in early 2027. The copy count sits beside it, so if you meant copies you can see that too.
  • Hobbit decks only is a separate toggle that stacks on top of the others. A deck qualifies by running at least one card new in The Hobbit. Reprints do not count, which matters: matching on set membership alone once read 344 of 479 decks as Hobbit decks, because every one of them ran a basic land printed in the set. A Hobbit land counts toward that badge while still costing nothing in wildcards or rotation.
