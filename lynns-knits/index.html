The maker

Lynn, one chair,
eleven years.

I knitted jumpers for ten years, then spent two years taking in repairs on other people's knitted bags. A bag that has gone baggy tells you exactly where the pattern was lazy.

Everything here is swatched, knitted, blocked, and lined by me in a room above a machine shop. A cable panel takes about eleven hours and a colourwork chart takes longer, so the studio finishes two or three bags in a week and no more than that.

The anime work started as commissions. People wanted a fox mask actually knitted into the fabric rather than printed on a canvas tote, so I charted my own motifs and put them in the shop.
