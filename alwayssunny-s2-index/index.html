Kid mode 🧒

This page is a list of the 10 episodes in Season 2 of the show It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia. The big new thing this season is that a short, loud, rich old man named Frank shows up and says he is the dad of two of the friends, Dennis and Dee. He moves in and starts causing trouble with them.

The Questions & Answers part at the bottom answers a tricky one: who is Dennis and Dee's real dad? The show keeps changing the story on purpose because it is funny. First Frank is their dad. Then their mom says a nicer man named Bruce is really their dad. But their grandpa on their mom's side is still their real grandpa, even so. The lesson is that a good list plus a few clear answers helps you keep track of a big, silly story.

Questions & Answers · recurring lore

Recurring story questions, traced across the series and grounded in the scripts. Click a question to open it. The flagship one starts right here in Season 2.

How does the story of Dennis & Dee's dad and grandfather evolve? spoilers thru S8

Season 2 hands you a father, then spends its finale taking him back. The show never fully settles the question after that, on purpose, and the murk becomes the joke. Here is the story, beat by beat.

S02E01 · Charlie Gets Crippled Frank Reynolds (Danny DeVito) reappears and gets introduced flatly as Dennis and Dee's father, a rich businessman who wants to quit the straight world and live like the Gang. The kids do not want him back.
"I wanna be the father that I never was."Frank, S02E01
"You can't waltz back into our lives and be our dad again!"Dennis, S02E01
S02E10 · Dennis and Dee Get a New Dad A man named Bruce Mathis contacts Dee claiming to be the twins' biological father. Cornered, their mother Barbara admits she had an affair with Bruce, got pregnant, and let Frank believe the twins were his. As of the season finale, Frank is not Dennis and Dee's biological father.
"There's this guy Bruce who contacted Dee on MySpace and he's claiming to be our biological father."S02E10
"Long story short, he got me pregnant, and I had to make a decision."Barbara, S02E10
"I decided that the best thing for everyone was if I didn't tell Bruce and let your father think that the twins were his."Barbara, S02E10
Frank shrugs off the blood question and re-adopts the Gang as his real family instead, the move that fixes him as the Gang's chaotic patriarch for the rest of the series.
"This is my adopted family." / "You adopted them?" / "Yup."Frank, S02E10
S03E03 · Dennis and Dee's Mom Is Dead Barbara dies, and her will puts the twist on paper: everything goes to Bruce, written in as the twins' real father. The show commits to the retcon rather than walking it back.
"I hereby leave all of your money to Bruce Mathis, the real father of my children."Barbara's will, read aloud, S03E03
S08E01 · Pop-Pop: The Final Solution Here is the elegant part. The paternity twist changed the father, never the mother, so the maternal bloodline stays intact. Barbara's own father, Heinrich Landgraf ("Pop-Pop"), an ex-Nazi, falls into a coma, and the estate points to Dee and Dennis as "the only living blood relatives" who can decide whether to pull the plug. Even without Frank's blood, the twins are genuinely Pop-Pop's grandchildren, through their mom.
"her father, Heinrich Landgraf, has fallen into a coma."estate lawyer, S08E01
"he was our grandfather."the Gang, on Pop-Pop, S08E01

The through-line. Frank stays "Dad" in every practical sense. He moved in, he pays, he never leaves. The biological question keeps getting re-opened by Bruce, then quietly proven beside the point in how the family actually works. The grandfather reveal is the show's sly reminder that the mom's side of the tree was real the whole time.

When and how does Frank actually join the Gang?

The Season 2 premiere, "Charlie Gets Crippled." Frank leaves the corporate world, forces his way back into Dennis and Dee's lives, moves in with Charlie, and starts bankrolling the bar. This is the show's single biggest change of shape: the four-person Gang (Dennis, Dee, Mac, Charlie) becomes the five-person Gang, and Frank's cash pays for half their schemes from here on.

"You can't waltz back into our lives and be our dad again!" / "And the money."Dennis, then Frank, S02E01

From the very first episode Frank acts like a fifth kid with his own checkbook, a role the finale (see the flagship question above) fixes in place permanently.

Why does Frank call Charlie and Mac his "adopted family"?

In the Season 2 finale, stung by the Bruce Mathis reveal that his biological kids may not be his, Frank pivots. Instead of fighting for Dennis and Dee, he declares the Gang, Charlie especially, his real family by choice.

"This is my adopted family." / "You adopted them?" / "Yup."Frank, S02E10

The show plays it as a punchline, and it fixes Frank's permanent place: the Gang's shared, feral father figure, the man who moves into Charlie's apartment and splits a pull-out couch with him for the next decade.

Who are the McPoyles?

The McPoyles are the Gang's inbred rival family, a clan of pale, milk-drinking creeps the show wheels out whenever it needs someone even worse than the Gang. The twins Ryan and Liam turn up in Season 1 and come back in Season 2 with their milk demands and incest gag intact.

"The McPoyle twins Ryan and Liam."S01E07, Charlie Got Molested
"Can we get a couple glasses of milk?" / "You can't have milk."S02E09
"We cannot have the McPoyle brothers in here creeping everybody out."S02E09

Their whole bit is family kept as a closed loop: warm milk, kept in the bloodline, everyone else in the room treated as the strange ones. That makes them a sharp foil for a show whose other big running question is who is actually related to whom.

What about Charlie's own father?

The twins get their big paternity reveal in the Season 2 finale, and the same stretch of the show quietly reminds you that Charlie's dad was never around either. Charlie's father has been in prison for Charlie's whole life, and Charlie says flat out that he never met the man.

"But he's in prison. He's been there my whole life."on Charlie's father, S02E10
"I never met my father!"Charlie, S02E10

The one father figure Charlie actually gets is Frank, who moves into Charlie's apartment in the season premiere and never leaves. The show keeps Charlie's real paternity a running blank instead of answering it, the same way it keeps the twins guessing.
