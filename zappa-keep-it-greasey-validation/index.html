The file stores loudness twice, as a ghost bracket and as a sticky beat dynamic. Resolving the dynamics in notated order needs no audio at all.

Mean dynamic rank 2.227 against 4.031, a gap of 1.804 ladder steps. Mann-Whitney z = -30.58, effect A = 0.0907.

Within-bar spacings: the flagged set sits 1 or 2 sixteenths apart on 62.89% of its gaps, the unflagged set on 35.88%. Permuting the ghost label among snare events inside each bar, 2,000 draws, gives a null mean of 0.5391. Observed is 1.167x the null with 0 of 2,000 draws reaching it. The ratio is modest and the separation is certain.

Two constructions agree without ever meeting.

The 2026-09-06 stem arbitration found the quiet snare population at 1 and 2 sixteenths and the loud at 3 and 5, from audio alone. The notation says the same thing from its own side, with no alignment between the two.

Whole-song kick-sequence DTW, criteria fixed before the run

The stability criterion was invalid as written. Fixed-endpoint DTW forces the remaining score across the same audio when an interior block is removed, which is why it reported 60 to 87 second disagreements. It is reported as a failure rather than quietly redesigned.

Split-half independent fits

Two maps fitted on overlapping halves disagree by 11.6 s across their 60 s overlap, with a range of only 441 ms. One fit has slipped by a near-constant lag. At 2.7 kick onsets per second that lag is about 31 events, close to the 32-event surplus.

6. Why a passing gate still places no note

All four of 18a2dd's criteria measure whether the retained runs agree with each other. None of them measures whether the runs are right.

Extending the same procedure across the song exposes it. Evaluating the identical slope gate at each window's centre instead of at bar 102 drops two windows to 0 of 9 retained and leaves one window keeping seven runs that span 2,066 ms. The gate is location-dependent.

Holding it to its own survivorship floor across a 110 s window leaves 14.0 s of usable map, which is 2.8% of the song, in two stretches: 193.50 to 197.00 s and 254.25 to 264.75 s. Six bars fall inside. The five contiguous ones were then scored under a window slid by minus one, zero and plus one bar, every shift measured on the same three interior bars so that no shift wins by dropping one.

7. The four findings

KIG-M001, bar 231, position 7 sixteenths: GM 65 on the kick staff line

One event in 4,948. It sits inside a repeating hat-plus-kick figure whose other string-4 events are all GM 35. The numbers 35 and 65 differ by one character. This needs no audio and no anchor, and it is the one candidate ready to apply.

KIG-M002, 21 GM 39 hand claps drawn at the snare position

Bars 22 to 32, 42 to 44, 65 and 82. All sit on staff line 1.5, so they draw as snare and sound as a clap. GM 37 side stick appears nowhere in the file, so GM 39 is this tab's only alternative snare-line articulation. The placement is certain and the intent is not recoverable.

KIG-M003, 114 ghost flags whose resolved dynamic is mf

Bars 104 to 105, 107 to 111, 119 to 122, 124 to 127, 130 to 142, 145 to 147, 186 to 187, 201, 227 and 231. The mark mf is the unflagged population's own, carried by 754 of its 794. The two encodings disagree and no evidence on disk says which is meant.

KIG-M004, 32 kick onsets with no notation

They sit 7.66 dB below the notated set's median, the smallest gap of any lane, and land on the grid at 1.646x baseline. That roughly 32 kick events are unnotated is supported. Which ones is not, because the anchor does not convert an audio time to a bar.

8. Bugs found in my own work

  • Dot and tuplet double-count. Songsterr's duration array already carries both, so applying them again pushed 88 of 248 bars past their own signature. After the fix all 248 balance.
  • A null that could not fail. The first grid test rotated onset times circularly, which preserves every inter-onset interval and returns the data. It scored 1.001x on every set including the loud ones.
  • A leave-one-out that could not work. Fixed-endpoint DTW cannot hold out an interior block.
  • A shift test biased by its own edges. Scoring shift +1 over one fewer bar lowered its total mechanically. Rescored on the interior bars only.
