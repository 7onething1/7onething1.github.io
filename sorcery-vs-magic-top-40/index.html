Rebuilds the game 1–6

Six differences that make Sorcery a different game rather than a Magic variant.

  1. The board is a 5x4 grid and position decides everything
    MagicThere is no board. Creatures exist in an abstract zone and location is undefined.
    SorceryTwenty squares. Every unit stands somewhere, and reaching a target is its own problem.
    Why it ranks 1. Nothing else in the game makes sense until this lands. It rewrites attacking, blocking, removal and threat assessment at once. Questing Beast · Sorcery: The TCG that banned digital art
  2. You bring two decks and choose which one to draw from
    MagicOne deck. Lands and spells are shuffled together and the draw decides.
    SorceryA 60-card Spellbook and a 30-card Atlas of sites. Each draw step you pick the deck.
    Why it ranks 2. This is the design's whole thesis. It removes the variance Magic treats as a core cost of play. Cardboard Guide · Sorcery in 2026: Is It Too Late to Get in?
  3. Mana screw and mana flood are engineered out
    MagicDrawing too few or too many lands loses games at every level of play.
    SorcerySites come from their own deck, so you take resources exactly when you want them.
    Why it ranks 3. The single most quoted reason Magic players switch. It changes what losing even feels like. Cardboard Guide · Sorcery in 2026: Is It Too Late to Get in?
  4. There is no stack, there is a Storyline
    MagicThe stack resolves last in, first out, and instant-speed responses stack on top of each other.
    SorceryEffects queue into a Storyline that resolves front to back in the order they were placed.
    Why it ranks 4. Every Magic instinct about holding up responses has to be relearned from scratch. Rule Zero · What's the Problem with Sorcery TCG (and Channel Updates)
  5. Threshold is a requirement you meet, and you never spend it
    MagicColoured pips are paid. A card costing two red consumes two red mana.
    SorceryThreshold checks how many sites of an element you control. Meeting it costs nothing.
    Why it ranks 5. One fire site lets you cast every one-fire card you draw. Curve maths from Magic simply does not transfer. SolarGames · My Thoughts on Sorcery TCG
  6. Every site pays you automatically, with no tapping for mana
    MagicYou tap lands one at a time to pay a cost.
    SorceryAll your sites generate one mana each at the top of your turn, with no tapping involved.
    Why it ranks 6. It removes a physical action Magic players perform hundreds of times per game. Good Time Society · Sorcery: Contested Realm - How to Game with Becca Scott

Rewires your turn 7–14

What you physically do between untap and pass changes here.

  1. Your Avatar is a card on the board that can be walked up to and hit
    MagicYou are an abstract life total that creatures attack directly.
    SorceryYour Avatar occupies a square, has power, moves, taps and can be cornered.
    Why it ranks 7. It turns the player into a piece, which is why every source reaches for chess as the comparison. Surgical Ninja · Is Sorcery TCG worth it in 2026?
  2. You must be on the same square to attack
    MagicAny untapped creature may attack the opponent from anywhere.
    SorceryA minion attacks what shares or neighbours its square, so threats need travel time.
    Why it ranks 8. Removal, tempo and racing all change once a threat needs two turns to arrive. SolarGames · My Thoughts on Sorcery TCG
  3. Unspent mana disappears at end of turn
    MagicUntapped lands stay available and let you hold up responses.
    SorceryMana you do not spend is gone when the turn ends.
    Why it ranks 9. The whole Magic habit of passing with mana open has no payoff here. Good Time Society · Sorcery: Contested Realm - How to Game with Becca Scott
  4. Killing takes two steps: death's door, then a death blow
    MagicZero life ends the game immediately.
    SorceryZero life puts the Avatar on death's door, immune for the rest of that turn. A later hit finishes it.
    Why it ranks 10. It hands the loser one full turn to answer, which reshapes every race in the game. Wizards' Den · How to play Sorcery TCG (2024) - Episode 01: Overview
  5. Attacking sites drains life without being able to finish anyone
    MagicAll damage to a player is damage and any of it can win the game.
    SorcerySite attacks make the Avatar lose life. Only direct damage delivers the death blow.
    Why it ranks 11. Two kinds of life loss that behave differently is the trap most new players fall into. Covenant · The Essential Sorcery TCG Tutorial
  6. Minion stats are symmetrical
    MagicPower and toughness vary independently, so 3/1 and 1/3 are different cards.
    SorceryA minion's single power number is its attack and its defense at once.
    Why it ranks 12. A whole axis of Magic design and evaluation is simply absent. SolarGames · My Thoughts on Sorcery TCG
  7. Interaction happens almost entirely on your own turn
    MagicInstants let you act at almost any moment during your opponent's turn.
    SorceryYour responses on their turn are Defend and Intercept, and little else.
    Why it ranks 13. Magic players describe this as the hardest adjustment after the grid. SolarGames · My Thoughts on Sorcery TCG
  8. Summoning sickness blocks tapping, and tapping is how you act
    MagicA fresh creature cannot attack or tap, and it can still block.
    SorceryA new minion cannot tap that turn, which blocks moving, attacking and tap abilities alike.
    Why it ranks 14. Because movement costs a tap, a new minion is far more inert here than in Magic. Archives of the Realm · Learn to play Sorcery! 5: Casting sites and spells with mana and threshold

Changes combat and the kill 15–24

Attacking, defending and winning all run on different rules.

  1. Minion damage clears at end of turn while Avatars stay wounded
    MagicAll damage clears at cleanup and creatures reset every turn.
    SorceryMinions heal at end of turn. Avatar life loss is permanent for the whole game.
    Why it ranks 15. Chip damage on minions is worthless, and chip damage on an Avatar is the entire plan. Covenant · The Essential Sorcery TCG Tutorial
  2. There is no limit to how many minions stand on one square
    MagicCreatures occupy no space and stacking is not a concept.
    SorceryAny number of minions can pile onto a single site.
    Why it ranks 16. It creates board states with real physical card-handling problems at the table. The Winning Agenda · How to Play Sorcery: Contested Realm TCG - Part 1 (The Basics) #sorcerytcg
  3. The void banishes anything that walks into it
    MagicThere is no terrain and nothing kills a creature for standing somewhere.
    SorcerySquares start as void. Most minions that end up there are removed from the game.
    Why it ranks 17. Terrain becomes a removal spell you never had to draw. Good Time Society · Sorcery: Contested Realm - How to Game with Becca Scott
  4. Combat resolves simultaneously with no blocker declaration step
    MagicThe attacker declares, then the defender assigns blockers in a separate step.
    SorceryFighters on a square trade at once, and defending is a move you make with Defend or Intercept.
    Why it ranks 18. The defensive skill in Magic is choosing blocks. Here it is choosing where to stand. Covenant · The Essential Sorcery TCG Tutorial
  5. Airborne is flying with diagonals attached
    MagicFlying only governs what can block the creature.
    SorceryAirborne units also move and strike diagonally across the grid.
    Why it ranks 19. An evasion keyword that also doubles your reach has no Magic equivalent. SolarGames · My Thoughts on Sorcery TCG
  6. Charge is haste, and it is far more valuable here
    MagicHaste lets a creature attack the turn it lands.
    SorceryCharge lets a minion tap immediately, so it can move as well as strike.
    Why it ranks 20. On a board where crossing distance takes turns, acting immediately is worth more. TCG TALK · Sorcery TCG Archetypes 101
  7. Lethal is deathtouch under another name
    MagicDeathtouch destroys any creature dealt damage by the source.
    SorceryLethal kills whatever it damages, no matter the target's power.
    Why it ranks 21. The mechanic carries over cleanly, and only the vocabulary changed. Rule Zero · Sorcery TCG is What Magic Players have been Looking For
  8. Ward means something completely different
    MagicWard taxes an opponent who targets the creature.
    SorceryWard in Sorcery is its own effect and the Magic reading will mislead you.
    Why it ranks 22. A false friend on a card you will read wrong on your first day. Covenant · The Essential Sorcery TCG Tutorial
  9. Targets lock when an effect enters the Storyline
    MagicTargets are chosen on announcement and checked again on resolution.
    SorceryA Sorcery effect fixes its target as it is placed into the Storyline.
    Why it ranks 23. Responses that save a creature by moving it work differently than a Magic player expects. Covenant · The Essential Sorcery TCG Tutorial
  10. Area of effect is measured in squares
    MagicA sweeper names a category of creatures and hits all of them.
    SorceryAn area effect hits the squares it covers, so geometry decides who dies.
    Why it ranks 24. Positioning becomes a defense against removal, which Magic never offers. Rule Zero · What's the Problem with Sorcery TCG (and Channel Updates)

Changes deckbuilding 25–32

Two lists, capped copies, and a format with no sideboarding.

  1. Rarity is a deckbuilding rule rather than a print run
    MagicFour copies of any card, with basic lands unlimited. Rarity only affects supply.
    SorceryOrdinary allows four, exceptional three, elite two, and unique one.
    Why it ranks 25. Rarity constrains your list directly, so the power ceiling is capped by design. Questing Beast · Sorcery: The TCG that banned digital art
  2. Deck construction is 60 spells, 30 sites and an Avatar outside the count
    MagicSixty cards total including lands, with a fifteen card sideboard.
    SorcerySixty in the Spellbook, thirty in the Atlas, a ten card Collection, and the Avatar is free.
    Why it ranks 26. You are building two lists at once and balancing them against each other. GOLDEN EAGLE CARDS · The Ultimate New Player Guide for Getting into Sorcery the Contested Realm! #newplayer
  3. The Collection is a third zone you reach into mid-game
    MagicThe sideboard is untouchable until games two and three.
    SorceryThe Collection is a ten card zone certain cards pull from during play.
    Why it ranks 27. A tutor target zone that lives outside the deck has no Magic analogue. GOLDEN EAGLE CARDS · The Ultimate New Player Guide for Getting into Sorcery the Contested Realm! #newplayer
  4. Best of one means no sideboarding in the Magic sense
    MagicMatch play is best of two out of three with sideboard cards between games.
    SorceryGames are best of one, so the Collection serves a different purpose entirely.
    Why it ranks 28. The entire skill of sideboarding does not exist in this format. GOLDEN EAGLE CARDS · The Ultimate New Player Guide for Getting into Sorcery the Contested Realm! #newplayer
  5. Sites carry abilities the way Magic's utility lands do
    MagicMost lands are basics that only produce mana.
    SorceryEvery site brings mana, a threshold symbol and often a printed ability.
    Why it ranks 29. Your mana base is thirty decisions rather than a curve calculation. SolarGames · My Thoughts on Sorcery TCG
  6. Card text is deliberately short
    MagicModern cards carry long rules text and heavy keyword loads.
    SorcerySorcery cards stay brief and the difficulty sits in the board instead.
    Why it ranks 30. Complexity moved from reading the card to reading the table. Rule Zero · Sorcery TCG is What Magic Players have been Looking For
  7. Mana spending is tracked in your head
    MagicTapping lands leaves a physical record both players can audit.
    SorcerySites do not tap for mana, so the count is spoken and remembered.
    Why it ranks 31. Reviewers name this as the game's weakest point for tournament play. SolarGames · My Thoughts on Sorcery TCG
  8. One set a year
    MagicMultiple premier sets a year plus supplemental products.
    SorceryEric's Curiosa ships a single set each year.
    Why it ranks 32. Your collection stays current for twelve months at a time. Surgical Ninja · Is Sorcery TCG worth it in 2026?

Changes the hobby 33–40

Art policy, release cadence, price and whether anyone near you plays.

  1. All art is physical media, with no digital and no AI
    MagicCard art is overwhelmingly digital painting.
    SorceryEvery piece is handmade in physical media, and digital art is disallowed outright.
    Why it ranks 33. The most cited non-gameplay reason players give for buying in. Questing Beast · Sorcery: The TCG that banned digital art
  2. Artists keep their copyright
    MagicCard art is typically work for hire.
    SorcerySorcery artists retain copyright, and originals and artist proofs stay theirs to sell.
    Why it ranks 34. A structural difference in how the game treats the people who make it. Distraction Makers · Finally, a TCG That Gives a S**t
  3. Many Sorcery artists are the old Magic artists
    MagicEarly Magic artists have largely moved on from the game.
    SorceryA large share of the Sorcery roster painted early Magic cards.
    Why it ranks 35. The visual lineage is deliberate and it is the pitch to lapsed players. ToGoBox · Collectors Are Leaving Magic The Gathering For Sorcery Contested Realm
  4. There is no spoiler season
    MagicFull set spoilers arrive weeks ahead of release.
    SorceryCards stay unrevealed, and Curios turn up in boxes unannounced.
    Why it ranks 36. Opening a box carries genuine discovery, which Magic traded away years ago. Rule Zero · Sorcery TCG is What Magic Players have been Looking For
  5. Alpha corners are a deliberate callback
    MagicModern printing uses standard corner radius.
    SorceryThe first print run uses the rounded corner profile of Alpha Magic cards.
    Why it ranks 37. A detail aimed squarely at players who remember 1993. SolarGames · My Thoughts on Sorcery TCG
  6. Products do not compete with each other for attention
    MagicReleases stack up and drown one another out across the same weekends.
    SorceryA single yearly release gets the room to itself.
    Why it ranks 38. Reviewers describe this as the difference between an event and a treadmill. Rule Zero · What's the Problem with Sorcery TCG (and Channel Updates)
  7. Sealed product runs far more expensive per box
    MagicA Magic booster box sits well under a hundred dollars at most retailers.
    SorceryGothic booster boxes were listed between 209 and 220 dollars on 2026-09-10.
    Why it ranks 39. Entry through the four-deck precon box is the sane route in. Collector Arthouse · Is Sorcery: Contested Realm Stagnating? Growth, Distribution & LGS Support
  8. Finding opponents is the real risk
    MagicMagic has a game in nearly every store, most nights.
    SorcerySorcery play depends on a local scene that may not exist near you.
    Why it ranks 40. Every source names this as the one thing that decides whether the game works for you. Collector Arthouse · Is Sorcery: Contested Realm Stagnating? Growth, Distribution & LGS Support

How the ranking was made

The ordering rule, stated so you can disagree with it.

  1. The question each rank answers How much does this change what you physically do on a turn? A difference that alters every decision outranks one that alters your shopping. The grid sits at 1 because nothing else in the game parses until you accept it. Finding opponents sits at 40 because it decides whether you play at all, and it changes nothing about the game itself.
  2. Sources 46 transcripts, 226,322 words, pulled with yt-transcripts and stored at ~/Projects/_outputs/yt-transcripts/sorcery-contested-realm-eval/. Rules claims were cross-checked across separate teaching videos before being written down. Where a source contradicted the rulebook walkthroughs, the walkthroughs won.
  3. One correction worth naming A popular tutorial says a summoned minion "can't do anything" on the turn it arrives. Six other sources state the actual rule: it cannot tap. Because moving and attacking both require a tap, the practical effect looks identical, and the printed rule is the narrower one. Entry 14 uses the narrower wording.
