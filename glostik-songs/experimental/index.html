    Request a song

    Want them to learn a song? Send the title and the artist. Your request goes on the list right away, and it gets checked off once the band plays it on stream.

    This is also fair warning: they may ask for a live learn, which means picking your song up on the night and working it out on stream.

    A donation is not required to ask. If you sent one, say so here and it reaches the band with your request. Send a tip

      Submit a correction

      See a song with the wrong name, the wrong band or the wrong time? Send the fix below. It goes to the person who keeps this list, and it gets checked against the video before anything on the page changes.

      Playlists

      A playlist is this same catalog with everything else taken out. Open one and the Songs tab shows only those songs, sorted and filtered the way it always is, each one still playing here on the page. The link is shareable.

      Make your own

      Type song titles, one per line, or name a genre. It matches them against the list and gives you a link to keep. It also sends the list to the band, so they can see what people want.
