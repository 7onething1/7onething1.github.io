# The Sorcery Player's Guide

Read across 46 Sorcery video transcripts (226,322 words). Every claim links to its source.

## 1. Before your first game
What to buy, and what that money really gets you.

### The four-deck box is the way in
The preconstructed box carries one deck per element and needs no deckbuilding. Cardboard Guide puts it around forty to sixty dollars.
Gothic booster boxes ran 209 to 220 dollars on a retailer check dated 2026-09-10, with everything under 180 sold out. Nothing about a booster box teaches you the game faster than the precon box does.
Source: Cardboard Guide, Sorcery TCG| New to Sorcery Contested Realm? Start Here! (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sVhKOGEoTBc)

### You will build two decks, and neither is optional
A constructed setup is sixty cards in the Spellbook, thirty sites in the Atlas, a ten card Collection, and exactly one Avatar. The Avatar sits outside both deck counts.
Games are best of one, so the Collection does a different job from a Magic sideboard.
Source: GOLDEN EAGLE CARDS, The Ultimate New Player Guide for Getting into Sorcery the Contested Realm! #newplayer (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CHjnsbbM2wE)

### Find the game before you buy the cards
Every source in the corpus names the same risk, which is having nobody to play against. A store owner quoted by Collector Arthouse ranked Sorcery last among all trading card games from a retailer's point of view.
Check the official Play Network for a store near you before spending on sealed product.
Source: Collector Arthouse, Is Sorcery: Contested Realm Stagnating? Growth, Distribution & LGS Support (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=H36hAhhXRqU)

## 2. Setup and the opening hand
Six cards, and a mulligan that costs you more than you expect.

### You draw three sites and three spells
The opening draw is three from the Atlas and three from the Spellbook, kept separate.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### The mulligan is three cards total, not three from each deck
You may mulligan up to three cards in any combination across the two hands. Sending back three spells means you keep all three sites.
Cards you mulligan go to the bottom of their own deck, and you redraw from that same deck.
Source: TCG TALK, Sorcery TCG Archetypes 101 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y86WzqtE37c)

### Your opening hand is a larger share of your Atlas than of your Spellbook
Three of sixty is five percent of the Spellbook. Three of thirty is ten percent of the Atlas. Your site draw is twice as representative as your spell draw.
TCG TALK estimates you see roughly fifteen to twenty Spellbook cards across a whole game. Build for the cards you will realistically draw.
Source: TCG TALK, Sorcery TCG Archetypes 101 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y86WzqtE37c)

### The second player gets a look
Competitive play lets the second player look at the top card of one deck and choose to keep it there or send it to the bottom.
Source: Game Brain Podcast, How To Play Sorcery: Contested Realm (with Matt and Jordan) (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0voIN8W3CrU)

## 3. The board
Twenty squares, and two range words you must keep separate.

### Adjacent and nearby are different words
Adjacent means the square a unit stands on plus the four squares orthogonally touching it.
Nearby means the full three by three block centred on that unit, so it includes the diagonals. Cards use both words deliberately.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Sites come in land and water
A site carrying a water threshold symbol is a water site. Everything else is a land site. Several keywords read off that distinction.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Three layers, and the void
Burrowing moves under land sites. Submerge moves under water sites. Voidwalk survives the empty squares.
A unit sitting in a region it cannot legally occupy dies. The void is a hazard rather than scenery.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Connected sites form bodies of water and terrain
Water sites touching each other count as one body of water, and cards read that whole body as a single thing.
Source: Questing Beast, Sorcery: The TCG that banned digital art (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=124O3PPg2q4)

## 4. The turn and your mana
Three phases, and a resource that behaves unlike land.

### Start, Main, End
There is no combat phase. Attacks happen during your Main phase, in whatever order your untapped units allow.
Source: Wizards' Den, How to play Sorcery TCG (2024) - Episode 01: Overview (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ONG7JGclej8)

### Every site pays you, automatically
At the start of your turn each site you control provides one mana. You never tap a site for mana.
A site you play mid-turn provides its mana immediately. Mana left unspent at end of turn is gone.
Source: Good Time Society, Sorcery: Contested Realm - How to Game with Becca Scott (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EGhE-3ov1RY)

### Threshold is checked, never spent
Threshold counts the elemental symbols on sites you control. Meeting the requirement consumes nothing.
One fire site lets you cast every one-fire-threshold card in your hand on the same turn, budget permitting.
Source: Archives of the Realm, Learn to play Sorcery! 5: Casting sites and spells with mana and threshold (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JYsYs6LnLts)

### Only your own sites count
An opponent's site never contributes to your threshold, even when your unit is standing on it.
Source: Archives of the Realm, Learn to play Sorcery! 5: Casting sites and spells with mana and threshold (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JYsYs6LnLts)

## 5. Moving and fighting
Tapping is the currency, and nearly every action spends it.

### Attacking means moving there and tapping
A unit taps to attack, and it must reach the target square. Move and Attack is a single action where the movement resolves first.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Defending costs a tap too
A defender taps and moves one square to intervene, and it must already be adjacent to the site it defends.
The defender absorbs the damage in place of the target.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Summoning sickness blocks tapping
A minion that arrived this turn cannot tap, which rules out moving, attacking and tap abilities alike. Charge removes the restriction.
Source: Archives of the Realm, How to play Sorcery Contested Realm with UPDATED rules for Gothic! (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RCQ_kC0Nvtk)

### Lance strikes first, then breaks
A lance grants plus one damage and striking first, then it is gone. Two lances give plus two, and both break.
When both fighters carry a lance, they trade normally and both lances break. Lances are artifacts, so a unit may drop them and another may pick them up, once each per turn.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

## 6. Spells that use geometry
Half the card pool cares where everything is standing.

### Projectiles travel in a line and hit the first legal thing
Magic Missiles fires three projectiles one at a time in one direction. Units sitting on the caster's own square are ignored.
Where several units share a square you choose which one each projectile hits, so three damage can be split across them.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Snowball collects what it passes
A snowball picks up every unit along its path and stops at the last site. It then deals damage to everything caught equal to how many units it caught.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Whirlwind rotates the board
You choose clockwise or counterclockwise, then every unit and artifact moves that way. It reshapes both armies at once.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Auras sit on the intersection of four sites
A normal aura is cast where four sites meet and affects all four. Wall of Fire is an exception, cast between two sites where you must control one.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Airborne changes who can touch it
Airborne units move and attack to nearby sites, diagonals included, and units without ranged cannot attack them. They can still be defended against, because they have to land to attack.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

## 7. Winning the game
Zero life is the start of the ending.

### Death's door comes first
An Avatar reduced to zero life is at death's door. It cannot gain life again and it is immune to damage for the rest of that turn.
Source: Sorcery News Network, How to Play Sorcery: Contested Realm |  Learn To Play In About 20 Minutes! (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VWDo4it7if8)

### The death blow has to be direct
After death's door, damage dealt directly to the Avatar finishes the game. Attacking sites makes the Avatar lose life without dealing it damage, so site attacks can never close.
Hold a direct-damage card for this. A one damage burn spell wins games that a board full of minions cannot.
Source: Covenant, The Essential Sorcery TCG Tutorial (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ss-PtdOBHcQ)

## 8. Archetypes, and why yours will not transfer
Aggro, control and combo describe a different game.

### There is no true aggro, only tempo
TCG TALK argues the aggro corner does not exist here. Charge, Lance and overstated vanilla bodies buy early tempo instead.
Bosstroll costs two with an earth threshold and attacks for three, which is the shape to look for.
Source: TCG TALK, Sorcery TCG Archetypes 101 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y86WzqtE37c)

### Control cannot hold up answers
Interaction on the opponent's turn is Defend and Intercept and very little else. You cannot counter, bounce or sweep in response.
The conversation happens through positioning across turns rather than through responses inside one.
Source: TCG TALK, Sorcery TCG Archetypes 101 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y86WzqtE37c)

### Value trading is the through-line
With so little card draw, the reliable plan is making the opponent spend two cards to answer one of yours. Area effects and tokens are the usual tools.
Source: TCG TALK, Sorcery TCG Archetypes 101 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y86WzqtE37c)

### Strike is how you attack without being hit back
Strike effects let an ally hit without the target hitting back. Whirling Blades moves an ally up to two steps and strikes every enemy along the path.
Source: TCG TALK, Sorcery TCG Archetypes 101 (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=y86WzqtE37c)

## 9. Mistakes new players make
Drawn from the rules questions that come up most.

### Reading nearby as adjacent
The diagonals belong to nearby only. Misreading this loses attacks you thought were legal and blocks defences you thought you had.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Walking your own units into a shark
A creature that fights whatever enters its body of water fights your units too. Check the water before you move through it.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Treating Genesis as optional
A Genesis ability triggers whether you want it or not unless the card prints the word may.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Expecting tokens to behave like minions after death
A token that would go to the cemetery is removed from the game instead. Plans that recur your dead minions skip tokens.
Source: The Void, 32 Common Rules Questions In Sorcery Contested Realm TCG (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PO1xzIhk_Vg)

### Reading Ward as the Magic keyword
Sorcery Ward absorbs one qualifying opposing interaction and then disappears. It taxes nothing.
Source: Covenant, The Essential Sorcery TCG Tutorial (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ss-PtdOBHcQ)

### Piling minions onto one site
Any number of minions may share a site, and the rulebook warns against it. One area effect answers the entire pile.
Source: The Winning Agenda, How to Play Sorcery: Contested Realm TCG - Part 1 (The Basics) #sorcerytcg (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=M2nEw-7euDk)
