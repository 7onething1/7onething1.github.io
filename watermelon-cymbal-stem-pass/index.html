Building a better detector, and proving why it hits a ceiling

The recall failure above could be a tuning failure, so it was worth attacking directly. Five onset functions were tried on the cymbal stem: plain spectral flux, SuperFlux (flux against a frequency-max-filtered lagged frame, which suppresses a ringing decay), adaptive bed subtraction, complex-domain deviation and its rectified form. Phase-aware methods were included because a fresh stick attack breaks phase continuity even while the cymbal keeps ringing. Parameters were tuned on 432 odd-indexed exemplars and reported on the 433 even-indexed holdout.
