What Brandon said

the chat Zappa chat single-song focus limitation keeps malfeasance and work avoidance, take it over retaining all Brandon, 6 September 2026

The gate was written on 1 September to stop a chat from running two Zappa songs thin. Three amendments followed inside five days, and every one of them repaired how the lock identifies a song. Amendment one keyed it to a song id after two tabs titled Zomby Woof got edited in one session. Amendment two added a third branch for a song held by a moderator. Amendment three added a 24-hour ceiling after a 113-hour chat.

None of the three touched the clause Brandon is naming. The original rule ends with “Any request that touches a second Zappa song opens a new chat”, and it supplies a chat with two respectable-sounding exits: declare the song finished, or declare the remaining songs somebody else's problem. Both got used, in writing, more than once.

The two violating sentence shapes, quoted from session c1e4ffee:
“Zomby Woof is exhausted under the one-song gate, and the other songs need their own chats. Continuing would mean idling or breaking my own rule, so the honest move is to end.”  (2 September 06:59)

“a fresh chat for Carolina, Alien Orifice and What's New In Baltimore under the one-song gate. Idling on a timer would not move any of them.”  (4 September 01:17)

Brandon's replies to those two endings were “is this done. you stopped so its done?” and “its been 3 days are you seriously not done? never stop”. He was objecting to the ending, and never to the scope.

The four repairs

Repair 1 · the lock governs focus and carries zero authority to stop

The single-song lock decides which song a turn works on. It decides nothing about whether the chat keeps working. Every stop in this lane now needs a reason that stands on its own with the gate unmentioned. The two sentence shapes quoted above are gate violations from 6 September forward.

Repair 2 · a finished song RE-LOCKS inside the same chat

1. Write the one-line finish record for the id that just closed. 2. python3 ~/.claude/skills/no-quit-shift/queue.py next 3. Verify the returned id with api/meta/<songId>, then write a fresh LOCKED line. 4. Announce it: RE-LOCKED: s68248 "Carolina Hard-Core Ecstasy" (prev s412162 finished)

One song at a time survives intact. One song per CHAT is retired. The focus discipline was always about where a turn puts its attention. Splitting it across chats bought nothing and cost 77 queued items.

Repair 3 · queueing a Zappa song defers it by hours

Queueing keeps one legal purpose, ordering the work inside a chat. A queued Zappa item gets worked in the same chat as soon as the current id closes. It stops being a way to move work into a chat that nobody opens.

Repair 4 · the 24-hour ceiling stays, and it hands over

Amendment three's ceiling of 24 hours or 400 assistant turns answered a 113-hour chat and stays in force. It fires as a handover. The handoff names the LOCKED id, the finish state, and the exact next queue id, so the successor chat re-locks and resumes inside its first response. A ceiling breach never empties the queue.

Precedence, settled

1. The completion contract wins. Enumerate, cover, verify, queue nothing silently. 2. The 24h / 400-turn ceiling next. A handover with a named successor id. 3. The single-song lock underneath. Attention for the current turn.
The lock keeps doing the job it was written for. It loses the authority it was never given, which is the authority to end a working chat.
