The absence of ~/.claude/agents and ~/.claude/worktrees is the evidence that those two routes have never run here at all. So "blocked" was the wrong word for three of the five: unused is the right one, and the difference between those two words is the thing Brandon has been pointing at.

And here is the likely reason the two machines behave differently at all. The config-sync installer states that CLAUDE.md is per-machine and is never written over, because it hardcodes the source Mac's home, drives and anchors. So the MacBook runs its own CLAUDE.md, which need not carry that one-session clause. The capability gap between the two Macs is most likely a single paragraph in a file that sync refuses to copy.

3. The structural defect behind all of it

The two skills that would have answered Brandon's question are among the ones switched off, and they were switched off for being unused. A sweep that judges a skill by how often it was invoked will always remove the monitoring layer, because a status dashboard is invoked rarely by design. The sweep removed this system's self-awareness, and then there was nothing left to report that it had.

4. The exact levers

Every one of these is reversible, and toggle.py moves directories rather than deleting them.

# see the fleet, without changing any policy
python3 ~/.claude/skills_disabled/toggle.py enable fleet-dashboard
python3 ~/Projects/fleet-dashboard/build_dashboard.py

# rank skills and connector adoption; do NOT run its deploy.sh on this Mac
python3 ~/.claude/skills_disabled/toggle.py enable system-pulse
python3 ~/.claude/skills/system-pulse/build_system_pulse.py

# restore the parallel-research skill from the trash
cp -R ~/.claude/skills/.trash/1792272791312-27828-YYTbyK/deep-research \
      ~/.claude/skills/deep-research

# allow one sub-agent spawn, 15 minutes, consumed on use
touch ~/.claude/.opus_ok

The one lever that is not a command is the first block: the one-session clause in ~/CLAUDE.md is Brandon's standing instruction, so sub chats stay off here until he changes that line. Nothing on this page was switched on while finding this out.
