This verse makes the body responsive to promise. Sarah receives strength where natural capacity had failed. The promise enters history through a human body that God empowers beyond ordinary reproductive limits.

**138. Hebrews 11:13, NKJV**

“They saw them afar off.”

This verse describes long-distance perception inside faith. The faithful saw promises from afar, embraced them, and confessed their pilgrim status. Their lives were shaped by realities they perceived without full possession.

**139. Hebrews 11:16, NKJV**

“They desire a better, that is, a heavenly country.”

This verse makes holy desire a sign of true origin. The faithful were drawn toward a country beyond the visible arrangement. God answers that desire by preparing a city for those who belong to Him.

**140. Hebrews 11:19, NKJV**

“God was able to raise him up.”
