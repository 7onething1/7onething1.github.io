How the sound is made. Equal temperament, A4 = 440 Hz. Each voice is a small additive stack (fundamental plus 3 partials) through an ADSR envelope and a lowpass, built with the Web Audio API. No samples, no network calls, works offline.

On the Perfect 5th. Your sheet jumps from Perfect 4th straight to Tritone, so the Perfect 5th has no line and no song on it. Its card is here because the ear-training set is incomplete without it, and it carries no mnemonic, since inventing one and attributing it to your sheet would be putting words in your mouth. Add the song you actually hear for it and the card can be filled in.
