Drum kit guitar-bleed audit

Band practice 8.28, four-mic master. Why the kick reads as a kick and the snare reads as a guitar. Measured 2026-09-04 on Brandons-MacBook-Pro.

Verdict

The skill transfer is intact. jam-stem-rebuild/SKILL.md on this Mac still carries the correct ordering at step 5 and step 6: Demucs first, DrumSep second, on the Demucs drums.wav. Nothing was lost from the other machine.

The 8.28 v2 run departed from that ordering. It fed a raw room mic straight into DrumSep, after boosting +5 dB at 2.5 kHz and +7 dB at 6 kHz, which is the exact band the guitar amps occupy. DrumSep was trained on already-isolated drum stems, so it had no way to reject the guitar. It sorted the guitar into the snare and cymbal lanes.

The good kits already exist on disk for 11 of 13 songs. Fixing this is hours, not three days.

3. Why it happened

  • DrumSep assumes a clean input. MDX23C-DrumSep was trained to split an isolated drum stem into six pieces. Its output heads sum back to the input, so anything in the input has to land in some lane. Guitar in, guitar out, distributed across snare and cymbals.
  • The presence lift made it worse. _kitsource_mic1_presence_lift.wav is mic 1 with +5 dB at 2.5 kHz and +7 dB at 6 kHz, applied to help the separator find cymbals. Electric guitar lives in that band, so the lift raised the guitar along with the cymbals. Measured: 41.6% guitar before the lift, 42.1% after.
  • The reasoning in the script was sound and the conclusion was wrong. The header of drumkit_from_mic.sh says “no separation upstream of this, so the kit split is the only model in the path,” treating the removal of Demucs as a provenance win. Removing Demucs removed the only stage that could take the guitar out.
  • The currently running chain is on the correct path. run_8_14_chain.py, live under launchd as com.drwu.chain-8-14-missing6, does Demucs, then measures the drum stem, then DrumSep. Leave it running.

4. The three-phase fix

Phase 1 — Adopt the kits that already pass, fill the two gaps

  • Promote the 11 existing Demucs-first kits to the delivered set, retire the mic-direct kits into a sibling delete/ folder rather than removing them.
  • Run do_song.sh 12 and do_song.sh 16 under launchd. Demucs is about 10 minutes per song, DrumSep is 80 to 90 minutes per song at num_overlap: 4.
  • Gate every lane on the air-band reality check from composite-stem-alignment/scripts/drum_lane_reality_gate.py so a hallucinated cymbal never ships.

Status: running since 2026-09-04T15:42:49 under com.drwu.phase1-song12-song16. Quarantine of the 13 contaminated kits completed at 15:44.

Phase 2 — Level and air, through the skill that already encodes it

  • The Demucs-first lanes measure 6 to 18 dB quieter than the mic-direct lanes. That is a gain problem, and it is separate from contamination.
  • multimic-stem-fix lesson 4 already restores the 6 to 16 kHz air DrumSep strips off hats and cymbals, by blending the highpassed full Demucs drums stem back in. Run the skill rather than hand-rolling an EQ.
  • Verify each restored lane against the raw mics with the air-band gate, so the restoration adds real signal.

Cost: minutes per song, scripted.

Phase 3 — The punchy version, proven on one song before any batch

  • Cancel the two guitar mics out of mic 1 first, then Demucs, then DrumSep. The prototype above already takes mic 1 from 41.6% guitar to 17.1% in 1.5 seconds per minute of audio, and keeps 96% of the kick band.
  • The payoff is a close-mic kit with the punch mic 1 has and the cleanliness the Demucs path has.
  • Prove it end to end on song 13 alone, measure the six lanes against the guitar mic, and only then run the other 12.

Cost: one 90-minute validation run, then a decision. This phase is optional and it does not block Phase 1.

5. Method and reproduction

Every number on this page comes from one measurement. For each frequency bin f, solve the least-squares gain a_f that best predicts the lane magnitude from the guitar mic magnitude, then report the explained energy as a fraction of the lane's total. The reference is always a verbatim guitar microphone, never a model output, so the test cannot be gamed by the separator that produced the lane.

A lag search from −3 s to +3 s was run on the Demucs-first kit to rule out the possibility that its low scores came from time misalignment. Best-lag snare was 2.2% against 1.9% at zero lag, so alignment is not carrying the result.
