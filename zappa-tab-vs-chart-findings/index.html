Tab against printed chart: five songs measured

Zomby Woof, Andy, Drowning Witch, Keep It Greasey and The Central Scrutinizer, compared bar by bar against professional drum transcriptions. Ghost and staccato marks ignored throughout. 2026-09-05.

The standard used here

One professional transcription outranks the tab. The Songsterr side is either a machine guess with a documented failure mode, or a human tab being checked. A printed chart is not held to a two-of-three vote against a machine.

The Atkinson chart cannot settle the meter, and the limit is the scan.

5. The Central Scrutinizer s3554079 hi-hat written at half density

Chart: Todd Bishop's groove-of-the-day post, one bar, 531 by 142 pixels. Tab: revision r4658080 by femboy.gardevoir, 92 bars.

This chart removes all lane guesswork.

It prints its own labels down the left edge: hihat, high/mid tom, snare, bass. No inference from staff position is needed, the first time that has been true in this series. Staff lines measured at y 76, 87, 98, 109, 119, spacing 10.8 px.

Chart 16 hi-hat strokes. Tab 8.

Chart heads measured above the top line at x 170, 190, 213, 235, 257, 279, 301, 323, 344, 378, 402, 424, 444, 466, 489, evenly spaced at about 22 px, which is sixteen sixteenth positions with one pair merged by the detector.

The tab's steady groove bars 4, 10, 11, 12 and 13 each read hi-hat 8, kick 6, snare 1 across eleven slots with durations 8 16 16 16 16 8 8 16 16 8 8. Across the whole part it holds 741 closed and 17 open hi-hat over 92 bars, an average of 8.2 per bar, so this is the tab's habit rather than one bar's slip.

Vinnie Colaiuta plays the hi-hat in sixteenths and the tab writes it in eighths.

The chart also marks accents on the hi-hat line and an o and a + above it. The tab uses the open lane 17 times in 92 bars and never uses the pedal lane. The kick and snare rows were not separated cleanly at 531 px width, so no claim is made on them.

What this says about the two kinds of tab

The two human tabs track their charts. Leonard's Andy tab closely: kick within one stroke across eight consecutive bars, and the same absence of hi-hat in the same place.

The machine tabs fail in a shape. Four byte-identical bars of tom and kick where the chart has snare and hat. Nine closed hi-hats where the chart has open and pedal. Zero grace notes in 115 bars. Kick and snare swapped on an off-beat. None of that is random error; it is a simplification repeated forward.

Method, so any of this can be rerun

  1. Identify the passage on three measures, meter, tempo and event count, never on position. Tempo can be measured off the record with librosa.beat.beat_track even where the record cannot settle a single note.
  2. Measure lanes, do not read them. Counting the bottom staff line as 0, kick is lane 0.5 and snare is lane 2.5.
  3. Build the subdivision grid from the hi-hat positions, not by dividing bar width.
  4. Validate every bar against its own meter before believing a count. Width over beats should be roughly constant; an outlier means a missed barline.
  5. Below about 15 pixels per staff space, do not trust notehead isolation on beamed music. Stems fragment beams into false heads. Read those by eye.
