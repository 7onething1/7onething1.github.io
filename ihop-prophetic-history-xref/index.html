Content gate, and a false alarm it raised

Each transcript was tested for its own subject rather than accepted on file size. Three initially read as empty. The cause was the model spelling Hebrew proper nouns phonetically: Hephzibah is rendered hefsabah, and Jehovah Rapha is rendered Rophe. Rerun on those variants, all ten carry their own subject. Session 7 contains 11 instances of the name, 13 of Isaiah 62, and 39 of "bride".

Session numbers declared inside the audio match the filenames where stated, at sessions 4, 5, 7, 8 and 10.

What remains open

  • Sessions 3, 6 and 9 have no canonical 2019 transcript. Session 3 is absent in every local format, and its 2009 twin IPH01 covers the same ground.
  • Three of the ten new transcripts belong to adjacent tellings rather than the 2019 run. The files numbered 03, 04 Joseph's Dungeon, and 06 Called to Proclaim retell IPH03, IPH04 and IPH06. They cover the subject matter of canonical sessions 5, 9 and 7 in an earlier voice, which makes the Joseph's Dungeon material readable even though session 9 itself is untranscribed.
  • Four 1988 transcripts already exist online at ihopnetwork.com in three formats, image PDF, OCR PDF, and plain text. Downloading beats re-transcribing.
  • The eight 2009 IPH PDFs still need text extraction. pdftotext is absent on this machine.

5. Where local and online disagree

This is the payoff of the cross-reference. The 1983 drought sign is the load-bearing miracle of the whole prophetic history, and the local telling and the published critiques do not describe the same weather.

What the local transcript says

From IHOPKC_s Prophetic History__7wk5hw5.txt, Bickle recounting Bob Jones speaking on May 28, 1983:

"there's a drought coming to this city. It'll be three months. And the drought is, is July 1st to October 1st."

He describes it as "the second worst drought on record" and reports the promised sign of rain arriving on August 23.

The revision sits inside the local file itself

The same passage carries an explicit adjustment of the start date:

"I think that day the drought started in June 1st, but when we looked at all the records, it was July 1st, October 1st."

Why that adjustment matters

The published critique attacks precisely the earlier window. Meteorological records cited in the Gruen material and in the Gospel Coalition assessment show above-average rainfall for June 1983 and average rainfall for July 1983, which contradicts a drought beginning June 1.

The local 2019-era telling places the drought at July 1 to October 1, moving the window past the month the records contradict. Both the original claim and the revision are preserved in the same local file, so the shift is documentable from Brandon's own corpus rather than only from critics.

What the local Sam Storms interview concedes

The one 2019 transcript held locally is the outside-voice session. It names the Arrowhead Stadium prophecy as the thing critics press hardest on, and it grants that Bickle "made mistakes like all of us do." It frames its own purpose as answering critics, which makes it the natural hinge between the internal telling and the external record.
