Their Secret Thoughts Cheatsheet, Chiron Voice

Each card keeps two lines: the real thought, then what is driving it underneath.

Cups

Ace of Cups
new offering, healing, test, gift
  • They want to offer you something brand new but fear rejection. They see you as the blessing that showed up right after the worst thing happened to them.
  • They're testing you before putting their whole heart on the table. They know you're the healing they need, just not ready to admit it out loud.
Two of Cups
options, karma, mutual connection, truth
  • They realize too late that you got options. They had a false sense of superiority thinking you'd just wait, and now the truth is coming out.
  • Karma is hitting right now. They tried to sell you a fantasy, but you stopped falling for fantasy and started falling for facts.
Three of Cups
reconciliation, scared, third party, mending
  • They want to reconcile but they're scared because of everything they pulled. They think you're going to be defensive and won't even hear them out.
  • Too many people influencing this connection. A third party energy has been all up in your business, and they know that's why things fell apart.
Four of Cups
rejection, stuck, unbothered, soul tribe
  • You ain't afraid of rejection. They can't rattle you and it's messing with their head.
  • They're stuck acting content when they're not. They want to act like they don't see the blessing being handed to them.
Five of Cups
sadness, trapped, light-stealing, grief
  • They want to trap you in sadness, take away your choices so you feel like you got no option but to give up.
  • They never dealt with their own grief, so they need you mourning right alongside them because your healing makes their stagnation look worse.
Six of Cups
nostalgia, inner child, past connections, deep bonds
  • They feel that nostalgic pull. The more people tried to hate on your connection, the more you turned it into love.
  • They want your healed inner child spark. You're tapped into something deep and familiar that they recognize from a past chapter.
Seven of Cups
illusion, fantasy, clarity, clouded vision
  • Their vision is completely clouded. They believed in the fantasy they created, but you ain't afraid to tell it how it is now.
  • They didn't see clearly and that ain't your problem. They're learning the path of forgiving themselves for being a big old dummy.
Eight of Cups
walking away, intuition, cut off, moving on
  • You walked away and it's haunting them. I ain't going to sit here all day in a pity party because you can't get it together.
  • Something got completely cut off. They didn't shoot their shot when they had the chance, and that window closed.
Nine of Cups
wish fulfillment, fighting feelings, manifestation, contentment
  • You got exactly what you wanted and they can't figure out how. They see you revealing harsh truths about people and they're absolutely floored.
  • They're fighting how they truly feel. Somebody's got to face it, and they know it's them. You are the wish fulfilled, not the obstacle.
Ten of Cups
complete connection, soul bond, manifestation, spirit confirmation
  • They're thinking about you from A to Z. They see this as a complete soul connection where love wins.
  • They are floored by what you can manifest together. Spirit is confirming this connection and they can feel it in their bones.
Page of Cups
apology, reaching out, intuition, emotional breakthrough
  • They want to reach out, have an emotional breakthrough. Your intuition about them was never wrong, never.
  • This is an apology card. They realized you were sitting there loyal the whole time while anxiety had them tripping.
Knight of Cups
protection, too late, divine protection, options
  • They realize too late that you are very protected. Spirit really does love you and will bring in new love just to prove it.
  • They realize a little too late that your emotions are protected and the fake love they showed got exposed the moment real ones stepped up.
Queen of Cups
grace, gift, acceptance, honesty
  • Something about you is a gift and they're mad about it. They can feel something is about to blow up in the open.
  • They need to be honest. They know you could handle the truth if they just told it, and your grace is what wrecks them the most.
King of Cups
emotional mastery, old ways, manipulation exposed, fear
  • They thought you were naive. They're realizing you can see right through the emotional manipulation, and wisdom without love is just a beautiful corpse.
  • Fear and anxiety causing them to act a fool. They're going from their old ways to a new way because something got put out into the open.

Swords

Eight of Swords

  • Something is eating them up and they are stuck and they don't know why. Wounds from the past keep them bound.

Nine of Swords
worried, anxiety, looping thoughts, options
  • You got options and it's giving them a headache. Worried about all the places you could go and all the things you could do.

Ten of Swords
cut off, betrayal, detachment, no celebration
  • You can detach like nobody's business. Once they betrayed that emotional softness, you retracted everything and it was done.

Page of Swords
new perspective, loyalty doubted, potential, waves in happiness
  • They doubted your loyalty from their own wounds. Now they're gaining a new perspective and seeing the truth about who you are.
  • They wanted to create waves in your happiness because things just come to you so easily and it makes them sick.
Knight of Swords
hiding something, not completely honest, rushing, eating them up
  • This person is not being completely honest about something and it's eating them alive. Something needs to come out.
  • They're not getting away with anything. You're going to keep rising no matter what they hide.
Queen of Swords
cannot be manipulated, cut them off, see right through, boss
  • You cannot be manipulated, period. You see right through them, and you will cut them off like no tomorrow.
  • They think you're naive and dumb but you clock every single move. Those who ostracized you are watching you thrive.
King of Swords
too intelligent, reverse Uno, cold, trying to get in your head
  • They're trying to break your mind, telling people you're cold, but the reverse Uno is that you're way too intelligent for their games.
  • They love creating drama and pinning people against each other. They love that they caused chaos and can't stand that it didn't work on you.
