# Which Zappa drum tab to redo first

All five records. Measured 2026-09-05.

## The five answers

**Apostrophe ('), redo Apostrophe'.** Five minutes fifty with no drum reading of the studio
take. The studio tab s35873 carries zero drum tracks, the AI tab is a 1974 Los Angeles live
performance of another night, and the only studio notation is Todd Bishop's two-system
excerpt of the Jim Gordon break starting at 3:22. Second is Excentrifugal Forz, whose only
tab is nine bars running 0:18 against a 1:33 track.

**Over-Nite Sensation, redo Dinah-Moe Humm.** Six minutes three of Ralph Humphrey that no
human has written down. Two AI passes both landed on the flattest lane profile measured on
any record here: 0.56 tom notes a bar against 5.5 hi-hat notes a bar. Fifty-Fifty is the
close second.

**Roxy & Elsewhere, redo Be-Bop Tango (Of the Old Jazzmen's Church).** Sixteen minutes
forty-two, the longest track across all five records, with nothing at all. Roxy is the
worst-served record in the collection: four of its ten tracks have no usable drum notation
and exactly one track, Echidna's Arf, carries a genuine human drum tab. If 16:42 is too big
a first bite, take Son of Orange County at 5:53, also at zero and instrumental throughout.

**One Size Fits All, redo San Ber'dino.** Five minutes fifty-nine with no drum notation of
any kind and Chester Thompson credited on drum set. Then Sofa No. 1 and Sofa No. 2, then
Florentine Pogen, whose human tab is a 112-note stub carrying zero hi-hat notes.

**Joe's Garage, redo He Used to Cut the Grass.** Eight minutes thirty-five with no drum
notation of any kind, and MusicBrainz credits Vinnie Colaiuta on drum set with Ed Mann on
percussion. It is the longest untranscribed Colaiuta performance in the collection. Then A
Little Green Rosetta at 8:15, then A Token of My Extreme, whose only tab is five bars
covering 0:15 of a 5:29 track.

## Two corrections to what was published earlier

**Village of the Sun does not have a human drum tab.** The earlier page listed s412175 as a
human Songsterr drum tab holding 402 notes across 43 bars. The note count is real and the
track is labelled Drums, and every one of those 402 notes sits on fret 1, 2 or 3. Songsterr
stores the drum lane in the `fret` field as a General MIDI drum number, so 1, 2 and 3 decode
to no drum instrument at all. That moves Roxy from two human drum tabs down to one, and
moves Village of the Sun into the redo-first group. The earlier page has been corrected.

**An off-map audit now runs on every measurement.** Village of the Sun was caught by
checking what share of each tab's notes fall outside the General MIDI drum map. Across all
thirty tabs measured, it is the only one at 100 percent off-map. The next worst are Crew
Slut and Montana at 5 and 4 percent, ordinary percussion colour like cowbell and tambourine.

## How the ranking was earned

Four signals, applied in order. First, is there any drum notation at all, paired with the
MusicBrainz drum credit so a track is only ranked first when the playing is confirmed
present. Second, has a human ever read it, taken off the `aiGenerated` flag in the meta API
rather than guessed from the author name. Third, notes per bar on a lane, used only to order
tracks that already tie on the first two, since a quiet tune genuinely has fewer tom hits.
Fourth, articulation marks as supporting evidence: nearly every AI tab measured carries zero
ghost notes, zero accents and zero staccato marks, and several human tabs also carry none,
so marks present means human and marks absent proves nothing. Keep It Greasey s604777 is the
outlier at 629 ghosts and 60 accents, the richest tab in the audit.

A caveat on Roxy: MusicBrainz carries zero instrument credits for that release at either the
recording or the release level, so drum presence there could not be confirmed from that
source and the ranking rests on the size of the gap alone.

Note counts exclude rests. Lane families follow the General MIDI drum map read off the
`fret` field. Tab durations are computed from the tempo automation and per-bar time
signatures, and album lengths come from the MusicBrainz release for each record.

## The metric that failed, recorded so it is not tried again

Bar-pattern uniqueness does not separate human work from AI work. Measured across fourteen
tabs, the human Zomby Woof tab is 36.8 percent unique bars and the human Inca Roads tab is
27.0 percent, and the AI Florentine Pogen tab is 85.5 percent and the AI Fifty-Fifty tab is
74.5 percent. The number tracks how repetitive the song is and says nothing about who wrote
the tab. It is excluded from the ranking.
