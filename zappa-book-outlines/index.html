How to use this with the four books. Benac gives you the spine of dates, personnel, and per-track commentary for 1966 to 1979. Watson covers everything Benac leaves out, from 1980 forward plus the archive and classical releases. Butcher supplies the household and working-method detail for 1967 to 1972 at close range. Moon Unit supplies the family cost of that same working method, from 1967 to now.

Overlap worth knowing. All four cover 1968 to 1971. Butcher and Moon both describe the Log Cabin and Laurel Canyon houses. Benac and Watson both cover every album from Freak Out! to Joe's Garage, so read them against each other where a fact matters.
