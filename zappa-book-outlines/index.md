## 2. Pauline Butcher, Freak Out! La mia vita con Frank Zappa

*Freak Out! My Life With Frank Zappa (2011), trans. Paolo Bassotti* | Italian | 130,797 words | 51 chapters | Eyewitness memoir  
**Span:** August 1967 to 1972, with an afterword running to 1997 | **Mining score:** 9/10

**Mine it for.** Butcher was Zappa's live-in secretary and typist from 1968 to 1971. She kept a diary and her mother kept forty years of her letters, so the book carries dated, first-hand domestic detail that no discography can supply. This is the richest seam here for scene, dialogue, and daily working method.

**Handle with care.** Italian translation, so any quote you lift needs checking against the 2011 English original before it goes in print.

## How to use this with the four books

Benac gives you the spine of dates, personnel, and per-track commentary for 1966 to 1979. Watson covers everything Benac leaves out, from 1980 forward plus the archive and classical releases. Butcher supplies the household and working-method detail for 1967 to 1972 at close range. Moon Unit supplies the family cost of that same working method, from 1967 to now.

**Overlap worth knowing.** All four cover 1968 to 1971. Butcher and Moon both describe the Log Cabin and Laurel Canyon houses. Benac and Watson both cover every album from Freak Out! to Joe's Garage, so read them against each other where a fact matters.

Extracted per-chapter plain text sits in the session scratchpad under `txt_butcher/`, `txt_watson/`, `txt_benac/`, and `txt_moon/`, numbered by spine order.
