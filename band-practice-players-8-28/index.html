How the takes were judged

The detector treats a false start as a take the band tried again, and compares each take head-to-head against the ones that follow using chroma-sequence similarity with lag alignment. Every one of the 12 keepers passed a from-scratch re-check measured against the room floor rather than against its own quiet passages. Spot-checking the cut list against the source, the kept takes average −8.0 dB and the dropped fragments −23.5 dB.
