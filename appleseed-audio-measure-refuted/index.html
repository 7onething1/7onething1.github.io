The Appleseed audio verdicts were a measurement artifact

29 of 46 songs were marked audio refuted on 2026-08-29 and queued for re-transcription. The measure that produced those verdicts was tested against 8 tabs typed by human transcribers for these same songs, where the answer is already known. It called 3 of them refuted too. Measured 2026-08-29, all numbers read from the project's own tools.

1. The verdict

Should the 29 refuted songs be re-transcribed, worst margin first?
No. That work order is sorted by a duration artifact.
The ordering correlates -0.861 with how much the winning control stem piled its hits onto a single onset, and that pileup correlates +0.850 with the control recording simply being shorter than the song. Confession heads the list because its control was 1.59× shorter rather than because its tab is the worst.
Were the 11 “audio supported” verdicts safe?
No. 7 of 11 flip to refuted.
Re-run with controls picked for duration instead of onset count, Reaction goes +8.6 to −47.2, Decline +14.6 to −26.4, Signal +9.7 to −23.0. The verdict depended on which control happened to be drawn rather than on the tab.
So what is established about these 46 tabs on audio?
7 confirmed, the rest undetermined. Nothing is refuted.
A validated measure confirms 4 machine tabs and 3 human tabs against their own recordings, with 0 of 162 known wrong pairings passing the same gate. Everything else is undetermined, which is not evidence of a bad tab: 5 of the 8 human tabs are undetermined too.

3. The mechanism

beat_map.py calls librosa.sequence.dtw(subseq=False), which forces the warping path to span both sequences end to end. The audit picks its control stems by onset count and never looks at duration. When a control recording is shorter than the song, the path has to compress the tab into it, and the compression becomes flat runs: hundreds of tab attacks interpolated onto one onset, every one of them counted as a hit inside the 30 ms window.

Every one of the 29 refuted songs drew a control shorter than itself. Every one of the 11 supported drew a control longer. A longer control forces the warp to stretch, which manufactures nothing. The verdict was decided by control selection before the tab was read. The existing collapse guard cannot see this because it tests the mean attacks per used onset against 2.0, and the pathology lives in the tail: Confession’s winning control averaged 1.56 while dumping 663 hits on one onset.

Constraining the warp does not help because it tightens both sides at once. At radius 0.01, Blind Man’s Arrow scores 79.0% against its own recording and 98.1% against a recording that is not its own. Once a monotone path is free to run over roughly 2,700 attacks and 1,200 onsets, it can fit a tab to nearly any recording, which is what beat_map.py’s own docstring warns: a warping path always exists.

5. The measure that survived

Alignment before any warping, with a ±3 s offset search offered equally to the own stem and to every control, scored as gain over that recording’s own chance level. It is asymmetric and is reported that way: a large gain confirms the tab is a transcription of that recording, a null gain is undetermined, because the tab’s tempo map may simply not track the performance. It never refutes.

The gate: z ≥ 5.0 over the recording’s own chance level and a gain at least 5.0 points above every duration-matched wrong stem. False positives: 0 of 162 wrong-stem pairings pass it. The largest gain any wrong pairing reached was +6.5, against +6.9 for the weakest confirmed tab.

The independent check: Blind Man’s Arrow and Mile Marker each have a human tab and a machine tab. Both transcriptions confirm independently against the same recording. Blind Man’s Arrow was one of the worst songs on the refuted list, at −22.9.

7. What is known, and what is next

Established.
  • The 29 refutations and the 11 supports are both artifacts of control selection. Neither direction was measured.
  • 4 machine tabs are positively confirmed against their own recordings, at 0 false positives over 162 known wrong pairings. Two of them, Blind Man’s Arrow and Doors Lead to Questions, were on the refuted list.
  • Playability is untouched by any of this. It was measured against the files themselves rather than against audio. Hard failures 1248 to 212, 15 of 46 hard clean. That result stands.
Not established, and left open on purpose.
  • Whether Confession’s tab is accurate. Its gain is -0.8 with a best wrong stem at +0.4. The measure cannot speak on it either way.
  • Whether re-transcribing from the stems would improve anything. It cannot be scored on audio with the tools in this project, so the re-transcription of Confession was not started. Spending it now buys an artifact nobody can grade.
  • Why 42 of 46 machine tabs come back undetermined. The most likely reason is a single constant BPM written into files whose performances move, which would put the pre-warp measure out of reach without saying anything about the notes.
