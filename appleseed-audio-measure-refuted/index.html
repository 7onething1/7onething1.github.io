Three clock bugs under the Appleseed audio verdicts

29 of 46 songs were marked audio refuted on 2026-08-29 and queued for re-transcription. Three separate defects were found underneath that number, each measured against tabs whose answer is already known. Every figure here is read from the project's own tools. 29 August 2026.

1. The verdict

Re-transcribe the 29 refuted songs, worst margin first?
No. That order is sorted by a duration artifact.
The margin correlates -0.861 with how far the winning control piled its hits onto one onset, and that pileup correlates +0.850 with the control recording simply being shorter. The same procedure refutes 3 of 8 human Songsterr tabs against their own recordings, and 7 of 11 supported verdicts reverse under duration-matched controls.
What actually moved the needle?
Fixing the bar-length bug. Confirmed machine tabs 4 to 8.
Reading each bar's length from its time signature instead of from its last note recovers a median 9.6 s per file, and takes confirmed machine tabs from 4 of 46 to 8 of 46 with none lost.
Did recording-derived tempo maps help?
No. They inflate the wrong pairings too.
All 46 files were written with per-bar tempo from their own recordings. Measured against its own negative controls the map is more permissive rather than more discriminating: the ceiling a KNOWN wrong pairing reaches goes from about +5 to +25.7, and at a threshold read off that ceiling 0 of 46 machine tabs confirm.

2. Bug one, control selection

librosa.sequence.dtw(subseq=False) forces the path to span both sequences, and the audit picks controls by onset count and ignores duration. A control shorter than the song makes the warp compress the tab into it, and the compression becomes flat runs: hundreds of attacks on one onset, all counted as hits in the 30 ms window.

3. Bug two, the reader's clock

attack_string_evidence.events() sets each bar's length to max(onset + duration) over that bar's note-bearing beats. A bar ending in a rest reports a short span, and the loss is carried for the rest of the song. Within-bar positions are fine; the bar cursor is not.

The written notation matches the recording to a median 0.9 s. The reader's version of the same file misses it by a median 12.5 s, worst 92.5 s. The notation was right and the clock reading it was wrong. The 8 human tabs drift 0.0 to 2.4 s because Songsterr exports carry no trailing rests, which was an unisolated confound in the first known-answer test on this page.

4. Bug three, the onset frame

beat_map.py takes its reference onsets from onset_detect(backtrack=True), which walks every detection back to the energy minimum before the peak. librosa.beat.beat_track puts its beats on the peaks. The two live in different frames.

−58 mssigned offset, 5 of 6 stems
2.6%beats hitting an onset
13.6%chance level
63.7%same test, backtracking off
A fixed −58 ms is 5 frames at hop 256. Beats hit the reference onsets less often than chance until the frame is corrected. Backtracking is kept, because a struck note starts at the rise rather than at the spectral peak and the tabs align to it: a human Mile Marker tab scores 56.9% against backtracked onsets at a 15.0% chance level. It is the beat grid that is moved, by each stem's own median offset, so no tab is consulted.

8. Confession, built and not promoted

Confession was re-derived end to end from 03_GUITAR_STEMS/Low Level Owl Vol II/12 - Confession.guitar.wav with basic_pitch on the ONNX model, quantised to a 16th grid at 174 bpm, and the fretboard solved with the skill's own beam. Nothing was carried over except the container.

Playability improved and audio accuracy did not: gain -0.8 to -0.3, separation -1.2 to -0.8, still undetermined. The file stays unpromoted. Its status in the queue reads audio accuracy undetermined, playability repair pending reliable timing alignment, with the old −36.6 kept only as a deprecated diagnostic.

Two things the build surfaced. A first pass clamped a fret to force a wide chord to fit, silently turning pitch 83 into 60 on 7 chords; the fallback now preserves every pitch and lets the beat fail honestly. And those 7 chords span up to 43 semitones, which is the two-guitarists-on-one-staff case this catalogue is full of.

Mare Vitalis track 7 is written here as And Nothing Less. The released title begins with an ellipsis and the filename on disk is unchanged.
