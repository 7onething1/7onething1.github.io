What was removed

The tape ran 3:45:27. These 22 takes account for 147 minutes of it. The other 79 minutes were 46 fragments, 9 stretches with no steady pulse, 5 passes of the band drilling one section, and 3 abandoned attempts. Six takes also carried a false start welded to the front, and each of those was cut back to the real downbeat.

The audio

Each player streams a 128 kbps AAC encode made from the 24-bit source in one pass, with no gain change and no normalization, totalling 136 MB. The lossless originals stay on disk at ~/Music/Band-Practice/songs-clean/. Every waveform is drawn from 1,600 precomputed peak and RMS buckets read off the 24-bit file rather than off the encode.

How these were chosen

The detector treats a false start as a take the band tried again, and compares each take head-to-head against the ones that follow using chroma-sequence similarity with lag alignment. The full method, the verdict counts, and the places where the evidence is thin are written up in the songs report.
