# Band Practice Players

Every complete take from the 8.14.26 practice tape, one player each. Built 2026-09-01.

Each page carries the same proven player used on `song-06-8-14`: canvas waveform drawn
from 1,600 precomputed peak and RMS buckets, scrub-to-seek, playhead, speed control,
volume, A/B loop, and keyboard shortcuts (space, arrows, J/L, A, B, 0).

## What is here

- 22 players, one per song
- 21 verified clean on a from-scratch re-check
- 146 minutes of music
- 6 takes had a false start trimmed off the front

## What was removed before cutting

The tape ran 3:45:27 and these takes account for 146 minutes. The other 79 minutes were
46 fragments, 9 stretches with no steady pulse, 5 passes of the band drilling one section,
and 3 abandoned attempts.

## Audio

Each player streams a 128 kbps AAC encode made from the 24-bit source in one pass, with no
gain change and no normalization. Waveforms are computed from the 24-bit WAV, not from the
encode. Lossless originals stay at `~/Music/Band-Practice/songs-clean/`.
