How a seed and a callback relate

A seed and a callback are one chain read from opposite ends. When a distinctive phrase turns up in more than one episode, its first appearance is the seed and every later appearance is a callback. That single structure produces all three views below.

What counts as distinctive

Raw word rarity cannot separate a signature term from ordinary speech in a corpus this size. Both spacetime and according appear about fifteen times. The separator used here is proper-noun-hood, measured by how often a word is capitalized in the middle of a sentence across the transcripts.

Chains are then restricted to referents outside the regular cast, since character names recur in nearly every episode and carry no callback signal.
