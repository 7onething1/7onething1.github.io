## Method

A seed and a callback are one chain read from opposite ends. A distinctive phrase's first episode is the seed; every later episode is a callback.

Distinctiveness is decided by proper-noun-hood, measured from mid-sentence capitalization in the transcripts, then restricted to referents outside the regular cast. Raw rarity cannot separate a signature term from ordinary speech in a corpus this size: both `spacetime` and `according` appear about fifteen times.
