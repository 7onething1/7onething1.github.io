# Community Character Map

An ancestry model for Greendale's ensemble, with a working index. Revision 7, September 15, 2026.

## The premise

NewsRadio is the organizing premise, the hypothesis that started the project, and it holds the strongest cast-level correspondence in the current scoring. Every show is scored by the same declared method.

- Dave Nelson to Jeff
- Joe Garrelli to Troy
- Matthew Brock to Abed
- Lisa Miller to Annie
- Beth to Britta
- Catherine Duke to Shirley
- Bill McNeal and Jimmy James to Pierce

Evidence labels: Episode, Commentary heard, Production record, Personnel, Published review, Interpretation. Commentary heard directly counts as evidence under its own label, with or without a transcript online.

## Saved by the Bell and NewsRadio into Greendale

The current focus is the line from Saved by the Bell and NewsRadio into Community. A commentary confirming that lineage has been found. It is rare, so the page records it as Commentary heard and does not link it. Every link below is new to this project unless its row says otherwise, and each is tied to dialogue, recaps, episode records or credits that the build checks on both sides. (Commentary heard)

The earlier finds, including "Jessie's Song", "The Zack Tapes", "Daydream", "Copy Machine" and "Station Sale", are on the [Greendale Lineage page](https://7onething1.github.io/community-greendale-lineage/). The Planbee handoff and the Annie line from Jessie to Frankie are on [that page](https://7onething1.github.io/community-greendale-lineage/#planbee) too.

No line of Community dialogue names either show, WNYX, Bayside or their lead characters. In Wikipedia's episode credits, Jeff Melman is the only director or writer on both a Saved by the Bell production and a Community episode, no NewsRadio director or writer is credited on Community, and seven NewsRadio guest stars plus one regular, Andy Dick, turn up at Greendale.

## Five kinds of ancestry

- Personality ancestry. Who a character resembles across the whole run. Example, Buffy to Britta.
- Relationship ancestry. A position held opposite one other character. Example, Angel and Buffy to Jeff and Annie, and Dave and Lisa to the same pair.
- Seasonal or functional slot. A job in the story that changes hands from season to season. Example, Buffy's rotating Big Bads to Chang.
- Developmental ancestry. One character moving through several older roles over time. Example, Troy, from A.C. Slater to Joe Garrelli, and Annie, from Cordelia to Willow to Dark Willow and from Jessie Spano to Lisa Miller to Frankie Dart.
- Shadow. An alternate, suppressed, future or replacement version of another character. Example, Annie Kim to Annie Edison.

## Troy's line

1. Slater stage (Interpretation, Production record). Troy arrives as the former high school football star. Harmon first pictured a young Woody Harrelson for the part and planned Troy and Pierce as the show's Beavis and Butt-Head pair.
2. Troy and Abed stage (Production record, Interpretation). Donald Glover and Danny Pudi's real-life friendship turned into comedic chemistry, shown off in the Spanish rap of the series' very first tag, and the show moved Troy from Pierce to Abed. The older models here are J.D. and Turk, and Joe and Matthew.
3. The repair gift (Episode). "English as a Second Language" (S1E24) reveals Troy's natural plumbing talent. Abed borrows the best-part-of-my-day speech from Good Will Hunting to push him toward plumbing, and Troy refuses and stays in school. On NewsRadio, Joe builds the camera hidden inside a Boba Fett doll himself ("Presence", S2E19, April 14, 1996).
4. Repairman stage (Episode). Troy trades his place in the study group for the Air Conditioning Repair Annex's help in "The First Chang Dynasty" (S3E21). In "Introduction to Finality" (S3E22) Vice Dean Laybourne tells him, "The true repairman will repair man."
5. The fire (Episode). "Intro to Felt Surrogacy" (S4E9) gives Troy the Greendale Fire of '03, 55 acres from one burned anthill. On NewsRadio, Joe starts the station fire by miswiring the new security cameras he meant to sabotage in "Led Zeppelin II" (S2E21, April 28, 1996).
6. Exit (Episode). Pierce's will leaves Troy his Hawthorne Wipes shares on the condition that he sail Pierce's boat, the Childish Tycoon, around the world ("Cooperative Polygraphy", S5E4), and in "Geothermal Escapism" (S5E5) Troy sets sail with LeVar Burton, assigned by the will to make sure he doesn't cheat.

## Shadows and succession

Community keeps doubles, futures and replacements of its own characters. Late regulars count on the Greendale side as a succession layer, so NewsRadio's Max Lewis matches Hickey, who arrives in Pierce's old slot in season 5.

- Annie Kim to Annie Edison, alternate, then rival, then replacement (Episode). Introduced in "Geography of Global Conflict" (S3E2) as Annie's multicultural evil twin with the same high school GPA, back in "History 101" (S4E1), and in "Queer Studies and Advanced Waxing" (S6E4) she replaces Annie as Daniel in The Karate Kid.
- Pierce to Jeff, future shadow (Interpretation). The ending Jeff fears if vanity, manipulation and the fear of becoming irrelevant win out.
- Frankie to Annie, professional endpoint (Episode, Interpretation). Frankie arrives as an administrative consultant in "Ladders" (S6E1) and already holds the list of problems Annie chased through season five. Andrea's arc on NewsRadio is the older model, where an efficiency review hands Lisa her boss's job. In "Basic Crisis Room Decorum" (S6E3) Frankie calls her apparent psychic streak an illusion created by being extremely prepared, and in the same scene she is told she sounds like Jeff, answers that Jeff said she sounds like Abed, and wonders whether Britta hears Chang. The Greendale Lineage page carries the full line.
- Hickey to Pierce's slot, succession (Episode, Interpretation). Buzz Hickey arrives in season 5 after Pierce leaves and appears in 11 of its 13 episodes. On NewsRadio, Max Lewis arrives in season 5 as Bill McNeal's replacement, so Max's reverse match is Hickey, rated 5 for ensemble function, with personality closer to 2.5 to 3.
- Elroy to Troy and Abed, recombination (Episode, Interpretation). Elroy and Jeff pull the Dean out of a 90s VR system in "Lawnmower Maintenance and Postnatal Care" (S6E2), and Elroy and Abed find a birds' nest in the router in S6E4.
- Chang to The series itself, role shadow (Interpretation). A character whose job changes whenever Greendale needs a new one, from teacher to student, guard, ruler, actor and tolerated outsider.

### How the working index is computed

Community Fit averages the nine Greendale slots. A cell with several approved names averages them. A source character used in more than one slot keeps its strongest use at full value, its second at 95 percent and its third at 85 percent. Tied uses share the average of their multipliers, so column order has no effect, and relationship positions don't count as a second use.

Source Fit averages each declared regular's best Greendale match on a separate reverse pass. Late Greendale regulars count through the succession layer, as Hickey does for NewsRadio's Max Lewis, and stage calls count at their stated rating, as Cordelia does for Buffy.

The working index is the harmonic mean of the two, scaled to 100 and rounded here.

Recomputing from the full map reproduces every published Community Fit score except Scrubs, which the tie rule moves from 4.62 to 4.61, and its index stays 93. Buffy's published 4.31 came from its map before the correction, which still reproduces it, and the corrected mapping replaces that row.

Each index is rounded once, from its exact harmonic mean, using Source Fit from the reverse roster where it is known. That puts Cheers at 80 from 80.47, where rounding the published 80.5 gave 81.
