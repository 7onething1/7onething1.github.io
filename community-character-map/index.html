The premise

NewsRadio is the organizing premise, the hypothesis that started the project, and it holds the strongest cast-level correspondence in the current scoring. Every show is scored by the same declared method.

  • Dave Nelson to Jeff
  • Joe Garrelli to Troy
  • Matthew Brock to Abed
  • Lisa Miller to Annie
  • Beth to Britta
  • Catherine Duke to Shirley
  • Bill McNeal and Jimmy James to Pierce

Saved by the Bell and NewsRadio into Greendale

The current focus is the line from Saved by the Bell and NewsRadio into Community. A commentary confirming that lineage has been found. It is rare, so the page records it as Commentary heard and does not link it. Every link below is new to this project unless its row says otherwise, and each is tied to dialogue, recaps, episode records or credits that the build checks on both sides. Commentary heard

The earlier finds, including "Jessie's Song", "The Zack Tapes", "Daydream", "Copy Machine" and "Station Sale", are on the Greendale Lineage page. The Planbee handoff and the Annie line from Jessie to Frankie are on that page too.

No line of Community dialogue names either show, WNYX, Bayside or their lead characters. Five kinds of ancestry

A Greendale character can take personality from one older show, a relationship position from another, a seasonal job from a third, and grow through several roles over the run. The show also keeps shadows of its own characters.

Personality ancestry

Who a character resembles across the whole run.

Example: Buffy to Britta

Relationship ancestry

A position held opposite one other character.

Example: Angel and Buffy to Jeff and Annie, and Dave and Lisa to the same pair

Seasonal or functional slot

A job in the story that changes hands from season to season.

Example: Buffy's rotating Big Bads to Chang

Developmental ancestry

One character moving through several older roles over time.

Example: Troy, from A.C. Slater to Joe Garrelli, and Annie, from Cordelia to Willow to Dark Willow and from Jessie Spano to Lisa Miller to Frankie Dart

Shadow

An alternate, suppressed, future or replacement version of another character.

Example: Annie Kim to Annie Edison

Troy's line

  • Slater stage InterpretationProduction record

    Troy arrives as the former high school football star. Harmon first pictured a young Woody Harrelson for the part and planned Troy and Pierce as the show's Beavis and Butt-Head pair.

  • Troy and Abed stage Production recordInterpretation

    Donald Glover and Danny Pudi's real-life friendship turned into comedic chemistry, shown off in the Spanish rap of the series' very first tag, and the show moved Troy from Pierce to Abed. The older models here are J.D. and Turk, and Joe and Matthew.

  • The repair gift Episode

    "English as a Second Language" (S1E24) reveals Troy's natural plumbing talent. Abed borrows the best-part-of-my-day speech from Good Will Hunting to push him toward plumbing, and Troy refuses and stays in school. On NewsRadio, Joe builds the camera hidden inside a Boba Fett doll himself ("Presence", S2E19, April 14, 1996).

  • Repairman stage Episode

    Troy trades his place in the study group for the Air Conditioning Repair Annex's help in "The First Chang Dynasty" (S3E21). In "Introduction to Finality" (S3E22) Vice Dean Laybourne tells him, "The true repairman will repair man."

  • The fire Episode

    "Intro to Felt Surrogacy" (S4E9) gives Troy the Greendale Fire of '03, 55 acres from one burned anthill. On NewsRadio, Joe starts the station fire by miswiring the new security cameras he meant to sabotage in "Led Zeppelin II" (S2E21, April 28, 1996).

  • Exit Episode

    Pierce's will leaves Troy his Hawthorne Wipes shares on the condition that he sail Pierce's boat, the Childish Tycoon, around the world ("Cooperative Polygraphy", S5E4), and in "Geothermal Escapism" (S5E5) Troy sets sail with LeVar Burton, assigned by the will to make sure he doesn't cheat.

Shadows and succession

Community keeps doubles, futures and replacements of its own characters. Late regulars count on the Greendale side as a succession layer, so NewsRadio's Max Lewis matches Hickey, who arrives in Pierce's old slot in season 5.

How the working index is computed

Community Fit averages the nine Greendale slots. A cell with several approved names averages them. A source character used in more than one slot keeps its strongest use at full value, its second at 95 percent and its third at 85 percent. Tied uses share the average of their multipliers, so column order has no effect, and relationship positions don't count as a second use.

Source Fit averages each declared regular's best Greendale match on a separate reverse pass. Late Greendale regulars count through the succession layer, as Hickey does for NewsRadio's Max Lewis, and stage calls count at their stated rating, as Cordelia does for Buffy.

Working index is the harmonic mean of the two, scaled to 100 and rounded here.

Recomputing from the full map reproduces every published Community Fit score except Scrubs, which the tie rule moves from 4.62 to 4.61, and its index stays 93. Buffy's published 4.31 came from its map before the correction, which still reproduces it, and the corrected mapping replaces that row.

Each index is rounded once, from its exact harmonic mean, using Source Fit from the reverse roster where it is known. That puts Cheers at 80 from 80.47, where rounding the published 80.5 gave 81.

  • Cordelia. Rated 5 as Annie's first stage, rivalling Britta, the same rating as Angel and Spike for Jeff.

  • Tie rule. Tied uses of one character share the average reuse multiplier, so column order has no effect. Scrubs moves from 4.62 to 4.61 and stays at 93.

  • Rounding. Each index is rounded once, from its exact value, so Cheers reads 80 from 80.47, where revision 2 rounded the published 80.5 up to 81.
  • Reverse concentration. The one-to-one match is the concentration check on both sides, since no character fills two slots and no slot takes two characters.
  • Reverse ratings. Exposed for fifteen rosters, seven more than revision 2.
  • Documentation. The AV Club line calling Joe and Matthew the proto Abed and Troy, "The Injury", both April Fools' Blank details and the review comparing Victorious and iCarly to Community now carry sources.
