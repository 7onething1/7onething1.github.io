What the number means, and what it does not

The test is presence, not identity. A song passes when some part carries exactly the expected ghost count. Live track names come back as empty strings from the API, so the drum staff cannot be named, only inferred. A part matching by coincidence is possible and unlikely at these values, and it is the honest limit of this measurement.

Three yardsticks were wrong before this one. Summing ghosts across all parts scored Watermelon 1,333 against an expectation of 1,317 and called it short, when the extra 16 sit on other staves and always did. Taking the largest part scored Black Napkins 24 against an expectation of 1, picking a guitar staff over the drum staff. Montana's 317 was two parts, 212 and 105, summed into one number. Each of those would have reported a healthy tab as damaged.
