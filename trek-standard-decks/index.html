How this was built. Channels resolved from your links, recent Standard videos pulled via the polite YouTube gate, then each video description parsed for the decklist link, the colors, the archetype, and whether it reads as a meta deck or a brew.

Where the price and wildcard numbers come from. Price is paper USD for the full list, summed from the cheapest Scryfall printing of every card, basic lands excluded, captured 2026-11-13. Paper prices move daily, so treat it as a snapshot for comparing decks against each other rather than a live quote. Wildcards are what MTGA would charge you after subtracting the 10,003 cards your collection export says you already own, bucketed by rarity. Both need the real 60-card list.

Buying a deck. Every deck carries a Card Kingdom button that opens their Deck Builder with the whole list already in the box. It is two presses from checkout, not a one-click cart: press Find Cards to price every line, then Add Items to Cart. Clicked through on 2026-08-25 and it reported Cards Selected 39/42, $274.91 on the first deck here. The button carries CK's own total when CK can fill every line; where it reads list instead, they are out of stock on at least one card, so the list still opens and the total is withheld rather than quoted short. The price above it is the cheaper of Card Kingdom and TCGplayer, and TCGplayer sits in the small print beside the local Kansas City option and the receipt.
