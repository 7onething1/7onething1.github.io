Seventeen Standard decks worked out across one long Magic conversation, each one showing how many rares and mythics you still need to finish it, after the cards already in your collection are subtracted.

Counts come from your Arena collection, snapshot 2026-11-03, 11,836 distinct cards. Every card below is matched against it across all Arena printings of a name, not one printing, then the copies you already hold are subtracted and only the shortfall is charged as a wildcard. Basics are free and never counted. Decklists are transcribed from the ChatGPT conversation White Liliana Preview with nothing added.

How a wildcard count is reached. For each nonbasic card, the copies already in the collection are subtracted from the copies the list calls for, counting every Arena printing of that name rather than a single printing, and only what is left is charged at the card's rarity. The headline pair is the 60 card list; where a sideboard exists its extra rares and mythics are stated beside it. Across all seventeen maindecks that comes to 142 rares and 54 mythics.

What is not claimed. Decks flagged short are short in the source conversation itself, and no card was invented to pad a list to sixty.
