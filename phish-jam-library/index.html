Production queue, in order

  1. All 17 stemmed jams have a published ixi page, each route verified live at HTTP 200. The 2025 West Coast run is fully read, Golden Age through Plasma.
  2. The deep vault · audited 2026-07-19. Of the 15 unstemmed jams, 12 have usable source audio: 11 of the 1994 to 2011 classics carry a clean instrumental.mp3 (264 minutes in total) and No Men in No Man's Land carries a 15:16 jam.mp3. Three have no usable audio at all: 1998-04-03 Nassau, 2003-02-28 Nassau Tweezer and 2010-06-26 Columbia hold only a narrated final_v2.mp3, which is the finished video soundtrack with voiceover over the music. The blocker is disk, twice over. demucs IS installed at ~/.venvs/demucs/bin/demucs, but torch never finishes importing with the Data volume at 98% (27 GB free), and a 6-stem WAV split of those 264 minutes needs about 15.6 GB against 7.8 GB free on the T7 Shield. Free space first, then split one jam per pass.
