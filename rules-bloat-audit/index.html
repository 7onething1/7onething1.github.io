The core behavioral gates earn their keep

The rules about grounding claims, never deleting, attributing stems, verifying live URLs and batching tool calls are the ones that show up repeatedly in the transcripts as real corrections. Keep every one of them. The bloat is in the long tail of single-incident captures around them.

Memory hygiene is better than it looks

Only 2 notes of 900 are orphaned from both indexes. The indexing discipline holds. The problem is volume and staleness rather than structure.
