3. Lines both shows say

Every phrase of five to eight words that appears in both shows and is rare in each, at most three episodes a side. Stock English is filtered out, and only the longest form of each echo is kept. The episode columns are where to check the claim.

A shared phrase is a starting point rather than a finding. Two comedies written in the same decade land on the same words sometimes. The rows worth your time are the long ones that tie a single episode to a single episode.

How this was measured

  • Community phrases: 649,696. NewsRadio phrases: 610,789.
  • Shared by both shows: 4,436.
  • Rare on both sides, three episodes or fewer per show: 3,916.
  • Carrying at least two content words: 1,842.
  • Longest form of each echo kept: 1,074 rows.
  • Every Community episode and every NewsRadio episode appears somewhere in the table.

The scripts behind this carry dialogue with no speaker names, so a row proves that a line was said in both shows and never who said it. Speaker attribution for Community sits in its own index, and for NewsRadio only 112 lines have a proven speaker.
