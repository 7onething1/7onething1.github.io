# Our Zappa Songsterr revisions: what matched a printed chart

Read from the revisions API, 2026-09-05.

## The answer

**Zomby Woof, and only Zomby Woof.** One tab out of eleven was corrected by us to match a
printed transcription. Six revisions on [s412162](https://www.songsterr.com/a/wsa/frank-zappa-tab-s412162) cite Ryan Brown's Groove
Analysis in DRUM Magazine, Daniel Bedard's handwritten chart, or the Drumnet drumset chart,
by name, in the revision description.

**None of them is published.** The live revision on that tab is Kirill's r8814965. Our
standing submission r8905491 is pending moderation, so the printed-chart corrections exist
as a queued revision and nothing more.

**Everything else we submitted was a different kind of edit.** Nine other tabs carry our
revisions and none cite a printed source. They are the 2026-08-29 ghost-note sweep, which
read Songsterr's parenthesised noteheads as a defect and stripped them, plus the restores
written to undo it.

**Moderation saved Keep It Greasey.** Our stripping revision r8768457 on s604777 carries
`isBlocked`, so a moderator rejected it and it never published. That tab still holds its 629
ghost notes and 60 accents, which is why it measures as the richest drum transcription
across all five records.

**A tab being ours is not the same as the transcription being ours.** On eight of these the
published revision sits under Brandon's name only because our sweep revision is the most
recent accepted one. The underlying transcription is someone else's work in every case, and
the pending restores exist to hand it back the way its author wrote it.

## Method

Revision history comes from `songsterr.com/api/meta/<songId>/revisions`, filtered to the
author name Brandon chavez, with the full description read for each one. A revision counts
as matched to a printed chart only when the description names the source: Ryan Brown, DRUM
Magazine, Groove Analysis, Daniel Bedard, or Drumnet. Live state comes from
`/api/meta/<songId>`, comparing `revisionId` against our newest submission, and `isBlocked`
identifies a moderator rejection.
