Stereogum’s Best New Music,
2017 Edition

Stereogum ran no ranked “best songs of 2017” year-end list. Their new-music coverage that year lived in one weekly franchise, The 5 Best Songs Of The Week, and in the rolling Spotify playlist fed by it. This page rebuilds that feed from the original posts, one week at a time, with every track carrying a link back to the Stereogum article it came from.

What this is

The target. Stereogum’s rolling “Favorite New Music” Spotify playlist held roughly one hundred tracks and was refreshed every Friday from that week’s five picks. To recover how it stood on June 16, 2017, take the twenty weeks ending that day. That is weeks #177 through #196, February 3 through June 16, and it comes to exactly 100 tracks.
How each track was verified. Every week below was fetched from stereogum.com by its own post ID, the publish date was read from the page metadata, and the five ranked entries were parsed from the article body. The dates run in an unbroken Friday sequence with no gaps.
The one open question. The playlist track count for June 2017 is not documented anywhere I could reach. One hundred is the working assumption, drawn from the size the playlist carries now. If it held a different number then, the boundary week moves and the rest of the reconstruction still stands, since it is built week by week rather than as a single block.

Get it into Spotify

Spotify has no public route to mint a playlist from a text list without account access, so the import runs through a converter. Both of the ones below take a pasted “Artist - Title” list and build the playlist on your account.
  1. Copy the block below, or download the .txt file.
  2. Open Soundiiz or TuneMyMusic and pick the text-import option.
  3. Paste, connect Spotify, name it, convert.
  4. Anything the converter misses has a per-track Spotify search button in the week listings below.
