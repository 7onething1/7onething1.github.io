Stereogum’s Rolling Playlist,
Spring 2017

Stereogum published no ranked “best songs of 2017” year-end list. Their new-music coverage that year ran through one weekly franchise, The 5 Best Songs Of The Week, which refreshed a rolling Spotify playlist every Friday. This page rebuilds the whole year from the original posts.

Start here

The February 3 to June 16 hundred is the main list. It is the one that sounds right to you, and it is the one the page leads with. Twenty weekly editions of five, ending on the June 16 column that carries Lorde’s “Sober” alongside SZA, Chelsea Wolfe and Emily Haines.
If you want the July songs too, take the 120. February 3 through July 14 keeps every track in the hundred and adds the four later weeks, which is where St. Vincent’s “New York” and Japanese Breakfast’s “Road Head” live. Nothing gets traded away to fit a round number.
Why the extension exists. A strict hundred-track window that reaches July 14 has to drop February, and February is where Mount Eerie, Jay Som, Vince Staples, Lana Del Rey and The Jesus & Mary Chain sit. The 120 keeps both ends.

The full 2017 run

Where the year starts and stops. The franchise opened 2017 with week #173 on January 6 and closed it with week #221 on December 15. That is 49 posts and 245 tracks, every week complete at five. Stereogum paused over the holidays and resumed January 5, 2018. The only Friday skipped mid-run was November 24, 2017, Thanksgiving week.
