The claim

The meter trap, stated because it nearly produced the wrong answer

Songsterr writes signature into a bar only when the meter changes. Reading each bar independently and defaulting to 4/4 reports this piece as 93 bars of 4/4 and gives 63.4% saturation with 1 fully saturated bar. Carrying the signature forward gives 89 bars of 3/4, 81.0% saturation and 75 fully saturated bars. Same file, same script, opposite verdict, no error raised.

Two checks catch it. Every bar’s durations must sum to its bar length, and the summed bar lengths at the stated tempo must land on the record’s runtime. The correct reading gives 2:38.3 against a 2:39 track. The wrong one gives 3:22.

What this page does not prove

Every number here measures the notation. None of them measures Chester Thompson. Proving that a written cymbal is absent from the record needs the recording split into separate cymbal lanes. A mixed Demucs drums stem holds kick, snare, toms, hat, ride and crash together and cannot say which one was struck; on Cosmik Debris that same shortcut produced two wrong verdicts that the six-lane split overturned.

The remaining work is queued: pull mgIK23Karm4, Demucs to a drums stem, run separator_resumable.py for the six-lane split, then score all 248 written cymbal beats against ride.wav and hh.wav.
