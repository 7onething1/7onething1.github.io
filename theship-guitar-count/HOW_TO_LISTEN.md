# How to listen, 12 clips, about two minutes

Each file plays in this order:

1. the real guitar, 1.6 s around the note
2. a reference tone at the pitch THE TAB WRITES
3. a reference tone at the pitch ALL THREE METHODS HEAR
4. the real guitar again

The filename carries both candidates, so `01_bar39_47.48s_tab-G2_vs_heard-B3.wav`
means the tab writes G2 there and pyin, the harmonic comb and basic-pitch all hear B3.

Say which tone matches, or say neither. Even three or four answers give me real-audio
ground truth to calibrate against, which is the one thing no measurement here could
produce. Nothing has been written into the tab.
