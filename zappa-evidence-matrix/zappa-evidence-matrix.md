# Zappa Evidence Matrix

Twenty six tracks across three albums, with direct drum evidence separated from structural
score evidence, so meter information is never mistaken for kit orchestration.

## The correction that reshapes the plan

The 1982 Frank Zappa Guitar Book carries the Steve Vai transcriptions from Shut Up 'n Play Yer Guitar, Joe's Garage and Sheik Yerbouti. None of these 26 tracks appear in it. It is a reference for Zappa's rhythmic notation language and tuplet practice, never a source for these songs.

## The seven evidence fields

| field | what it licenses |
|---|---|
| **FULL DRUM** | A complete independent drum-kit transcription of the track |
| **PARTIAL DRUM** | A drum staff covering part of the track only |
| **OFFICIAL STRUCTURAL SCORE** | Album book, note-for-note. Gives bar geometry and ensemble attacks, never kit orchestration |
| **ACADEMIC SCORE** | Thesis or dissertation score, sometimes carrying a real drum staff |
| **PERFORMER SOURCE** | Written by the drummer who played it |
| **MATCHED PERFORMANCE AUDIO** | A specific recording or film the tab can be aligned against |
| **RED ZONE RHYTHM** | Tuplets, simultaneous subdivisions, nested tuplets or rapidly changing odd meters. No automated repair permitted inside these ranges |

## The rhythm red zone rule

Every passage carrying quintuplets, sextuplets, septuplets, octuplets, nonuplets, ten-note
groupings, simultaneous meters, nested tuplets or rapidly changing odd meters is marked
before any hit-level comparison. The same marking applies wherever the structural
transcription shows drums reinforcing a melody accent or an ensemble attack.

**No automated repair inside a red zone from neighbouring drum context alone.**

Establish the exact bar duration from the album book or academic score, then the fractional
attack grid from the written ensemble parts including documented ratios such as seven
against six, then the kit assignment from a direct drum transcription, performance footage
or drummer source. Compare Songsterr against that using rational beat positions rather than
a fixed sixteenth grid.

## The three critical red zones

**Inca Roads.** drums play SEPTUPLETS against bass sextuplets, then a QUINTUPLET against marimba sextuplets, in the same bar, over a sustained 4/4 pulse

**Don't You Ever Wash That Thing?.** opening figures 4/16, 5/16, 9/16, 11/16 with equal 5/8 percussion passages between. 11/16 groups as 3+3+5, 9/16 as 4+5.

**Be-Bop Tango.** beat 1 a QUINTUPLET, beat 3 a SEPTUPLET subdivided 2+2+3, both against the continuing pulse

## Inca Roads is the calibration target

Benjamin Wall's thesis carries a complete study score transcribed from the record, explicitly
including drum kit, percussion, timpani, bass, marimba, keyboards, guitar and vocals, written
because no suitable complete score existed. That gives real drum-lane evidence alongside the
surrounding ensemble parts, so a suspicious drum event can be tested for whether it
independently follows the groove or copies a composed ensemble attack. It is free.

The difficult material is exactly what the validator has to survive: drums playing septuplets
against bass sextuplets, then a quintuplet against marimba sextuplets, in the same bar, over a
sustained 4/4 pulse. The One Size Fits All book independently supplies the interlude meters,
11/16, 8/16, 7/16, 5/4, 2/4 and 4/4.

## What each book is for

**Over-Nite Sensation and One Size Fits All, the bar skeleton source.** Note-for-note across
every track on their albums. They supply section boundaries, time signatures, bar lengths,
tempo changes, written tuplets, rests and exact attack positions wherever the kit clearly
doubles an ensemble figure. Extract that into a machine-readable rhythm map before the next
large correction pass.

**The 1982 Vai guitar book, notation language only.** None of these 26 tracks are in it. It
carries Zappa's high-complexity rhythmic vocabulary and transcription conventions, so it is
precedent for how sixes, sevens, nines and conflicting subdivisions get represented rather
than flattened into ordinary sixteenths.
