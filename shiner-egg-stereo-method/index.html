Reading a record the way these analyses do

Source documents: 6 theory analyses (Shiner × 3, Hooray for Earth, Phish, plus a riff tab sheet) · Worked example: Shiner, “The Truth About Cows”, The Egg, 2001 · 201 s of audio · 8 of 10 written claims confirmed straight from the mix

These documents share one habit that makes them work: they read the stereo field as structure. “Gtr 1 (right ear), Gtr 2 (left ear)” is the analytical premise the rest of the page rests on. That habit is also the one thing the existing stem pipeline throws away, because Demucs returns a single mono guitar stem and the two parts are gone before the analysis starts.

What this page is. The written analysis is treated as the answer key and the audio as the input. Every claim below became a measurement with an honest verdict, including the two that the full mix cannot settle. Nothing here is asserted from listening or from memory. Where a measurement cannot isolate a claim it is marked untested, not quietly upgraded.

The gap this exposes

Demucs is the wrong first step for this kind of reading. Six-stem separation collapses both guitars into one mono guitar.wav. Every observation in these documents about two parts, about double-tracking, about one guitar playing G lydian against the other’s G aeolian, is unrecoverable after that step. The stereo pass has to run on the mix first, and it costs nothing: no torch, no model download, no separation time.

The order that works

1. Stereo field on the raw mix: L, R, mid, side, windowed correlation. This is what tells you how many parts there are and where they split.
2. Root motion by persistence on the mid channel. Sections fall out of this.
3. Mode tests per chord window, one distinguishing pitch class at a time.
4. Then stem-separate, for the things that genuinely need an isolated part: vocal melody, tuning, per-part transcription.

What it cannot do, stated plainly

Tuning. Drop-D cannot be read off a full mix. The bass guitar lives in the same register, so the low band cannot attribute a note to an instrument. This needs a guitar-only stem.

The G section. The written descent B minor → A minor → G minor reads the guitar parts. The B and A centres are unambiguous in the low register. The bass never settles on G, exactly as the document implies when it describes the two guitars pulling against each other there, so a full-mix test corroborates two of the three and should say so.

Anything about the vocal. The missing-third move, where the melody supplies a quality the chord omits, is the sharpest idea in the Shiner “Pills” analysis, and it needs the voice on its own.

Running it

python3 stereo_field.py <song.wav> --out field.json
python3 claim_test.py  <song.wav> claims.json

Both scripts need only librosa, numpy and soundfile. No torch, no model weights, no network.
