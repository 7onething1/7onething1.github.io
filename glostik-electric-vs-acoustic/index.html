How each column was decided

Two different kinds of claim sit on this page, and they are kept apart.

Measured. The stream log labels its own nights. Four of the 98 streams are the garage run, three of them titled Electric:

  • 2026-09-28 Electric, Live From Our Garage, Live With Jon (37 songs, 4.6h)
  • 2026-09-30 Electric, Live From Our Garage (21 songs, 3.71h)
  • 2026-10-04 Electric, Live From Our Garage, With Jon (19 songs, 2.23h)
  • 2026-10-05 Live From Our Garage (29 songs, 3.87h)

106 of 3,874 plays on record happened on those four nights, so a song being garage-only is weak on its own at that sample size. What the log does prove is which songs were played with the electric rig up, and that is the garage badge wherever it appears below.

Judged. Everything in the second and third sections is my call on the song, which means a distorted riff, a named electric tone, a solo that carries the arrangement, or a part the acoustic duo flattens. Call those read-and-argue, not measured.
