How the region was set each time

  • Bar positions came from the set file, not from pixels. The .als is gzipped XML. Parsing AudioClip for CurrentStart and CurrentEnd gives every song's exact extent.
  • Every song has one clip spanning its whole length on the Kick track. Clicking that clip's title bar sets the time selection to its exact extent, and the Export dialog inherits it.
  • Read back before rendering. Render Start and Render Length were confirmed on screen for all 11 before Export was clicked, then the finished duration was checked against the bar count.
  • Exact extent instead of padding. A mis-aimed pad click can shorten a song, so each render used the clip's own boundaries.
