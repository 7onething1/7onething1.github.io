# Community continuity ledger, pass four, transcript-verified

Every entry below was tested against a transcript first. The evidence base is eighteen episodes extracted from the BluRay discs on this machine, 7,828 timecoded subtitle cues. Each claim carries an episode code, a timecode, and the line as the subtitle renders it. Where the transcript refuses a claim, the refusal is recorded beside the claim.

One limitation governs the whole pass. Subtitle tracks carry no speaker attribution, so any statement about who says a line is marked inferred from placement. A speaker-tagged transcript would settle those, and the episodes lacking local video remain outside evidence entirely.

## The Spanish 101 opening is one continuous stretch, not five scattered seeds

The ledger had been treating Abed's television framing, Britta's reality question, the courtly greeting, and Jeff's entrance as separate seeds inside a dense episode. The timecodes show them occupying about ninety seconds of a single unbroken sequence. Abed responds to the Dean's announcements at 00:00:46 by saying it makes every ten minutes feel like a new scene of a TV show. He then dismantles his own framing at 00:00:54 by observing that the illusion ends once somebody says how much life is like television.

Britta's challenge lands at 00:01:57, phrased as a question about whether he understands that television and life are separate things. The courtly greeting follows at 00:02:04. Jeff's entrance closes the unit at 00:02:15, where Abed grades the performance and Jeff answers that he staged it for Abed specifically.

Reading those five beats as one sequence changes what the episode is doing. The show establishes Abed's television grammar. It tests that grammar through Britta, demonstrates it through Jeff, then confirms that Jeff performs inside it on purpose, and the third minute has not arrived. Status: structural observation, confirmed by timecode across S01E02 cues 20 through 59.

This strengthens the existing claim rather than weakening it. Pierce's reaction to his mother's death draws on a belief system the series had already built, priced, and mocked. Status: confirmed callback, upgraded from thematic echo, S01E12 cues 101 through 106.

## What the evidence base can and cannot settle

Subtitles give exact wording. They give exact timing. They give no speaker. They give no stage direction, and no camera information. Every visual claim in the ledger, including the background childbirth, the RoboCop display, the buried study table, plus the costume continuity, sits outside what this corpus can test.

The honest split is therefore three ways. Wording and timing claims can be confirmed or refused here. Speaker claims can be supported by placement and marked inferred. Visual and staging claims need a different evidence layer, and labeling them confirmed on the strength of a subtitle would be a false citation.
