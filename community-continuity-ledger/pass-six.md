# Community continuity ledger, pass six

Pass five caught one negative result that turned out to be a broken probe rather than an absence. That raised an obvious question about the other negatives, so all eleven were re-run under a second, deliberately loose pattern. The audit is in `reaudit.py`, and it changed the ledger in both directions.

## Four negatives flipped, and one of the flips was my own bad probe

Running each claim twice, once strict and once loose, produced four apparent reversals. Three are real. The fourth is a lesson.

The Chang ventilation claim appeared to flip, with twenty-three hits across twelve episodes. Inspecting them killed it. The loose pattern searched for `vent` and `duct` as substrings, so it matched inside "events", inside "product", and inside unrelated dialogue about a ceiling. Not one hit referred to a ventilation system.

The original negative therefore holds, and the correction runs against my own method rather than against the show. A loose pattern manufactures false positives exactly the way a strict pattern manufactures false negatives. Status: negative upheld, flip withdrawn as a probe artifact.

## The triple-cool count was three, and it is four

Pass five recorded three instances of the triple-cool tic and treated that as the corrected figure. The loose re-run found a fourth in "Aerodynamics of Gender" at 00:03:42.

The four sit in "Home Economics" at 00:07:09, "Aerodynamics of Gender" at 00:03:42, "Critical Film Studies" at 00:16:02, and "Competitive Wine Tasting" at 00:12:25. A doubled version also appears in "Home Economics" at 00:10:32, which is recorded separately rather than counted with them.

Correcting a correction is worth stating plainly. Pass four said zero, pass five said three, and the count is four. Status: confirmed, four instances, S01E08 through S02E20.

## Five negatives held under both patterns

These returned nothing under a strict pattern and nothing under a loose one, across all twenty-three episodes: the pile-of construction, the Magnitude catchphrase, the six-seasons line, the streets-ahead joke, and any dialogue naming a Winger speech as a Winger speech.

Holding under two independent patterns is a stronger result than holding under one. These five are recorded as absent from the range this corpus covers, and the absence is now evidence rather than an artifact.

The Winger speech result carries the most weight. The show's most recognizable structural device is never named aloud by the characters anywhere in these twenty-three episodes, which places the self-aware naming of it later than the stretch in evidence.

## The rule this pass adds to the method

Every negative result now needs two probes to enter the ledger. A strict pattern establishes the claim as worded. A loose pattern tests whether the strict one was doing the work.

A loose hit gets inspected first and only then counts, because substring matching finds words inside other words. Three of this pass's four flips survived inspection and one did not.

Silence only becomes a finding after both probes return nothing. Pass four broke that rule and published a conclusion drawn from a single broken pattern, which is the error this method exists to prevent.
