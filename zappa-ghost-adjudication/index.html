Zappa Ghost Adjudication

Not "put all the ghosts back". Which ghosts the printed transcriptions support, song by song and lane by lane, so the genuine ones return and the invented ones stay gone.

The rule that decides it A ghost notehead is standard notation for a quiet snare stroke between the loud ones. On a snare lane a ghost flag carries its ordinary meaning and is presumptively genuine. On ride, hi-hat, tambourine, kick or crash it means something else, so it needs a printed chart to justify keeping. That single test splits the 4,574 removed flags almost in half.

01The three songs already settled show the whole spread

Each landed differently, which is exactly why a blanket restore would be wrong.

Watermelon In Easter Hay s35881 — KEEP ALL 1,317, on the ride

Zero snare ghosts. Every flag sits on ride, which normally reads as the wrong lane. The placement settles it: slots 0, 4, 8 and 12 are 407 plain against 2 ghosted, and every other sixteenth is 1 plain against 1,314 ghosted. That is an accent grid, the soft rides between the accented ones. A ghosted ride is alone in its beat 99.4% of the time, and every note reads dynamic:null, so the strip revision's "at forte" justification was invented.

Zomby Woof s412162 — DROP ALL 32, ADD 8 THAT WERE NEVER THERE

The 32 automatic detections sit at bars 4, 13, 15 and 40 to 43, with 22 of them on hi-hat lanes where ghost brackets are not valid notation. Drumnet prints genuine parenthesised snare pairs at bars 5 to 8, slots 6 and 7, right after the open hi-hat, and the transcriber missed every one. Zero overlap between what was flagged and what is printed. This song has the richest chart coverage of all 23, and its ghosts turned out to be the wrong ones.

Montana s35870 — RESTORE 317 AND REMOVE THE DOTS

A later revision converted all 317 ghosts into staccato dots. A dot marks duration and a ghost marks dynamic, so the substitution is wrong whichever ghosts prove genuine. The dots come off in the same pass.

03What each class needs next

Snare only, no chart needed to proceed

Nanook Suite 168, Nanook Rubs It 168, Catholic Girls 16, Zoot Allures 53, Keep It Greasey 629. Every flag is on snare, where ghost notation carries its ordinary meaning. These can be restored on the notation standard rather than on a chart, and Zoot Allures plus Keep It Greasey are already safe because moderators rejected their strip revisions.

No snare at all, chart required

Watermelon 1,317 is settled as genuine. Carolina Hard-Core Ecstasy 144 on tambourine and Oh No 141 on closed hat are not settled, and Carolina has no chart on this Mac at all. Those two are where a wrong blanket restore would do the most damage.

Muffin Man 504 snare against 74 other, Drowning Witch 101 against 318, Inca Roads 134 against 31, Alien Orifice 33 against 53, What's New In Baltimore 4 against 55, The Black Page 170 against 3, Fembot 53 against 4. The snare half restores on the standard. The remainder needs the chart, and Ryan Brown covers Drowning Witch and Muffin Man while Drumnet covers Muffin Man and Oh No.

Five songs have no chart on this Mac

Nanook Rubs It, Nanook Suite, Carolina Hard-Core Ecstasy, Fembot In A Wet T-Shirt, Catholic Girls. Four of the five are pure snare so they proceed anyway. Carolina is the one genuine blocker, since 144 tambourine ghosts cannot be judged without a printed source.
