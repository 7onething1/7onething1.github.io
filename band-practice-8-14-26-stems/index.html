How these were made

The tape is mono and it carries most of its energy in the low end, outside what Demucs was trained on. Separating it raw drops the guitar stem into bass register, measured at 93.9 percent below 250 Hz on song 04. So the mix gets a corrective tilt first, then separation runs, then the inverse tilt goes back on every delivered stem so the tone matches the source.

modelsupplieswhy
htdemucs_6sguitar, otherthe only model with a guitar head
htdemucsbass, drums, vocalsfour heads hold more capacity per source
Two guitars, one stem. The channels are bit identical, so the recording carries no pan cue. Both guitarists come out inside one guitar.wav, and no register split was applied, since that would be an inference dressed as a measurement.
A silent drum stem is an empty chair. The drum chair rotates through this band, and on some takes that player moved to second guitar or to bass. Both models agree on those takes, so the silence is the room and not the separation.
