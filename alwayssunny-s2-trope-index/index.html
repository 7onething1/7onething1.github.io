Season 2

S02E01 - Charlie Gets Crippled

  • Logline: Frank Reynolds turns up at Paddy's the same night Dennis runs Charlie over with a car, announces he is divorcing the twins' mother and giving his whole fortune to charity so he can live like his kids, and Dennis and Dee drop everything to loot his apartment before the money is gone, wheeling the newly crippled Charlie around as a pack mule.
  • Tropes used:
    • Bomb-then-soften "business tactic": Frank's opening move is to tell the twins their mother is dead, then walk it back to a divorce, calling it a negotiation trick ("You drop the bomb, then you soften the blow"). His amoral logic is set in his first scene.
    • Renounce-the-fortune inciting device: Frank hands the episode its engine by announcing he will give his money to charity and live like the Gang ("I'm giving all my money to charity", "I wanna be the father that I never was").
    • Greed over reconciliation: the twins ignore the reunion and fixate on the cash, talking over Frank's speech ("You can't waltz back into our lives and be our dad again!", answered with "And the money").
    • Escalating sibling contest: Dennis and Dee turn stripping Frank's apartment into a literal race ("Whatever your feeble little arms can carry out of here, you can keep"), then use Charlie's wheelchair to haul loot ("Go, horsey!").
    • Exploit the injured friend: the Gang trades on crippled Charlie for free pity lap dances at the strip club ("His friend ran him over with a car", "I could give you one for free"), then puts him to work as manual labor.
  • Tropes subverted:
    • The "estranged father comes home to reconnect" setup gets flipped: the kids want the inheritance, and the reunion itself means nothing to them.
    • The disabled-friend sympathy that usually earns care instead turns Charlie into a prop and a cart.
    • Frank's give-it-all-to-charity redemption never happens; the family loots him and he stays exactly the man he was.
  • Keywords: Frank Reynolds debut, divorce, wheelchair, charity, looting, strip club, lap dance, sibling rivalry, business tactic, inheritance grab
  • Mirrored subplots: The cold-open race (Dennis and Dee competing over drinks and a strip-club trip) rhymes with the main plot (Dennis and Dee racing to grab Frank's possessions); both use Charlie as the instrument that decides the contest.
  • Callbacks: none (season premiere). First appearance of Frank Reynolds, and the first mention of the twins' mother Barbara and the divorce.
  • Pays off in: Frank's arrival resets the series into a five-person Gang. The divorce and Barbara set up "Mac Bangs Dennis' Mom" (S02E04), and the family-money and paternity threads pay off in "Dennis and Dee Get a New Dad" (S02E10). Frank moving in with Charlie begins their permanent roommate pairing. Full dad and grandfather timeline in the Season 2 index Q&A.
  • confidence: high, grounded in the S02E01 transcript.
