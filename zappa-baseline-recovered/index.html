The shared-reference trap, in the open

Those 36 tied events come from 6 distinct Note elements. Notes 788 to 791 render twice each in bar 16. Notes 793 and 794 carry every open hi-hat tie across all ten bars. Dropping the tie from note 793 changes ten bars at once. Here that is the intended result, and the auditor is what turns it from an assumption into a count.

What that does and does not establish

It does establish that the parenthesis rendering is absent from the GPIF articulation layer of the file r8787022 was built from.

It does not establish that Songsterr's renderer showed no parentheses, because Songsterr draws from its own track JSON. That payload sits behind CloudFront and returned 403 AccessDenied to an unauthenticated fetch this session, with and without browser headers, on the original revision r7115188.

The two descriptions contradict each other

r8766102 says it removed the 32 parenthesised noteheads. r8787022, one day later, says it converted the same 32 to staccato dots. The same 32 noteheads cannot be removed and then converted. Total events held at 7,479 across both, so nothing was deleted either time. One of the two descriptions does not match what its revision did.
